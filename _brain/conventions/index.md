# Conventions

Standing rules for this implant. A convention is the decided answer to "how do
we do this here" — read these before writing anything.

* [Addressing scripture](addressing-scripture.md) — a verse is addressed by an
  atomic reference token (`jhn14v6`), because the search tokenizer cannot tell
  `John 14:6` from `John 6:14`. The retrieval key that makes finding content by
  verse work at all.
* [Reporting, not endorsing](reporting-not-endorsing.md) — the neutral point of
  view rule: we prove that somebody *said* something, never that what they said
  is true. What lets every tradition share one repository.
* [Written to be public](written-to-be-public.md) — every page is addressed to
  a stranger on the internet, and no text we may not redistribute enters it.
* [Every claim carries its receipt](every-claim-carries-its-receipt.md) — no
  statement of fact without a resolvable citation attached to that statement.
* [Evidence before authority](evidence-before-authority.md) — a claim is
  weighed by its evidence, never by who made it.
* [Credence is a model, not a measurement](credence-is-a-model-not-a-measurement.md)
  — a probability here is the output of a stated, replayable model, never a
  claim to have measured a truth.
