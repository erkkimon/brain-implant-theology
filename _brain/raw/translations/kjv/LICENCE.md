# King James Version (1611/1769) — licence

**Status: public domain. Free to redistribute.**

## The text

The King James Version was first published in 1611; this is the 1769 Oxford
standardised edition, the one in ordinary use. Both are far beyond any
copyright term based on the life of the author plus seventy years.

## The one caveat, and why it does not apply here

In the **United Kingdom only**, the KJV is subject to a perpetual Crown
copyright, exercised through letters patent and administered by Cambridge
University Press as the Queen's/King's Printer. That is a peculiarity of UK
law with no equivalent elsewhere.

Everywhere else — including **Finland**, where this repository is produced —
the text is unambiguously public domain. Recorded here so the reasoning is
auditable rather than assumed, and so anyone republishing this material from
the UK knows to check their own position.

## Verification

`John 14:6` in this edition reads:

> Jesus saith unto him, I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh
> unto the Father, but by me.

which matches the standard 1769 text.
