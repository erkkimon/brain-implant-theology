## A context-window failure ate a turn, and the transcript was the recovery

Turn 18 of the session that was building the spine answered Tom's
MVP-first instruction, did the work (book articles, testament roots, chapter
up-links in the theology implant), and then the session died: the model
switched from a 1 M-token context to a 98 k one, the request returned
`prompt exceeds context`, and the automatic compaction failed with the *same*
error — so there was no summary and no turn 19. The work on disk survived;
only the conversation was lost.

Recovered it from the session transcript itself, which the harness keeps as a
zstd-compressed JSONL event log per session (12 023 records here):
decompressed it, found the user message, replayed the assistant's steps and
their tool calls from the chunk stream. Worth recording as a procedure — when
a session is corrupted, the JSONL is still the ground truth of what happened,
and the tail records tell you exactly where it stopped (`turn/end` with an
error reason).

## MVP first: what "lovable" means, and the order it happens in

Tom's instruction, and the decision that follows from it: **the two implants
reach a minimum lovable product before any translation tokens are spent**, and
improvement then proceeds in cooldown-sized daily batches. Recorded as the
plan [plans/mvp-then-iteration.md](../plans/mvp-then-iteration.md); mirrored in
the apologetics implant.

Three things were settled, not just noted:

1. **The spine.** One article per biblical book, chapters joined to it, each
   book bound to its testament article. Apocrypha stay *outside* the spine —
   ordinary articles that link to canon, never a load-bearing rung. The spine
   lives here and is shared with apologetics through federation, using the
   documented `[[target]]{brain://brain-id}` suffix form from the
   [federation spec](https://github.com/benquemax/brainpick/blob/main/docs/federation.md).
2. **Finnish is shippable, and why.** Semantic search is multilingual, so
   Finnish content answers English queries. That is what licenses the
   Finnish-first order — it is not a compromise, it is the reason the gate can
   be this early. Which makes `bge-m3` (free, multilingual) load-bearing, not
   optional: the currently detected `nomic-embed-text` is English-only and
   would silently break the whole premise.
3. **Machine time before token time.** Conversion, link parsing and spine
   generation cost machine hours and no tokens, so they run first and make the
   brain navigable immediately. Translation and improvement are the daily
   loop afterwards, one *themed* slice per burst — themed rather than
   alphabetical, so each burst finishes a coherent sub-domain instead of
   leaving fifty half-touched ones.

Measured rather than assumed on the way: the ApoWiki export holds
**6 798 wiki links in 845 of its 848 files** (and 156 of them are redirects),
so the initial interlinking really is a parsing job rather than an LLM job —
Tom's hypothesis checks out against the data. The files are `.wiki`, not `.md`;
an earlier `*.md` glob reported "no links" and was nearly the basis for a wrong
plan. A glob that matches nothing is not evidence of absence.
