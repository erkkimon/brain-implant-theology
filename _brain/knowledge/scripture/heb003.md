## KJV — Hebrews 3

### Hebrews 3:1 `heb3v1`

Wherefore, holy brethren, partakers of the heavenly calling, consider the Apostle and High Priest of our profession, Christ Jesus;

### Hebrews 3:2 `heb3v2`

Who was faithful to him that appointed him, as also Moses was faithful in all his house.

### Hebrews 3:3 `heb3v3`

For this man was counted worthy of more glory than Moses, inasmuch as he who hath builded the house hath more honour than the house.

### Hebrews 3:4 `heb3v4`

For every house is builded by some man; but he that built all things is God.

### Hebrews 3:5 `heb3v5`

And Moses verily was faithful in all his house, as a servant, for a testimony of those things which were to be spoken after;

### Hebrews 3:6 `heb3v6`

But Christ as a son over his own house; whose house are we, if we hold fast the confidence and the rejoicing of the hope firm unto the end.

### Hebrews 3:7 `heb3v7`

Wherefore (as the Holy Ghost saith, To day if ye will hear his voice,

### Hebrews 3:8 `heb3v8`

Harden not your hearts, as in the provocation, in the day of temptation in the wilderness:

### Hebrews 3:9 `heb3v9`

When your fathers tempted me, proved me, and saw my works forty years.

### Hebrews 3:10 `heb3v10`

Wherefore I was grieved with that generation, and said, They do alway err in their heart; and they have not known my ways.

### Hebrews 3:11 `heb3v11`

So I sware in my wrath, They shall not enter into my rest.)

### Hebrews 3:12 `heb3v12`

Take heed, brethren, lest there be in any of you an evil heart of unbelief, in departing from the living God.

### Hebrews 3:13 `heb3v13`

But exhort one another daily, while it is called To day; lest any of you be hardened through the deceitfulness of sin.

### Hebrews 3:14 `heb3v14`

For we are made partakers of Christ, if we hold the beginning of our confidence stedfast unto the end;

### Hebrews 3:15 `heb3v15`

While it is said, To day if ye will hear his voice, harden not your hearts, as in the provocation.

### Hebrews 3:16 `heb3v16`

For some, when they had heard, did provoke: howbeit not all that came out of Egypt by Moses.

### Hebrews 3:17 `heb3v17`

But with whom was he grieved forty years? was it not with them that had sinned, whose carcases fell in the wilderness?

### Hebrews 3:18 `heb3v18`

And to whom sware he that they should not enter into his rest, but to them that believed not?

### Hebrews 3:19 `heb3v19`

So we see that they could not enter in because of unbelief.
