Verses are addressed by atomic reference token — `psa45v1` is Psalms 45:1 — because the search tokenizer splits `Psalms 45:1` into separate terms and cannot then tell it from `Psalms 1:45`. See [addressing scripture](../../conventions/addressing-scripture.md).

## KJV — Psalms 45

### Psalms 45:1 `psa45v1`

To the chief Musician upon Shoshannim, for the sons of Korah, Maschil, A Song of loves. My heart is inditing a good matter: I speak of the things which I have made touching the king: my tongue is the pen of a ready writer.

### Psalms 45:2 `psa45v2`

Thou art fairer than the children of men: grace is poured into thy lips: therefore God hath blessed thee for ever.

### Psalms 45:3 `psa45v3`

Gird thy sword upon thy thigh, O most mighty, with thy glory and thy majesty.

### Psalms 45:4 `psa45v4`

And in thy majesty ride prosperously because of truth and meekness and righteousness; and thy right hand shall teach thee terrible things.

### Psalms 45:5 `psa45v5`

Thine arrows are sharp in the heart of the king’s enemies; whereby the people fall under thee.

### Psalms 45:6 `psa45v6`

Thy throne, O God, is for ever and ever: the sceptre of thy kingdom is a right sceptre.

### Psalms 45:7 `psa45v7`

Thou lovest righteousness, and hatest wickedness: therefore God, thy God, hath anointed thee with the oil of gladness above thy fellows.

### Psalms 45:8 `psa45v8`

All thy garments smell of myrrh, and aloes, and cassia, out of the ivory palaces, whereby they have made thee glad.

### Psalms 45:9 `psa45v9`

Kings’ daughters were among thy honourable women: upon thy right hand did stand the queen in gold of Ophir.

### Psalms 45:10 `psa45v10`

Hearken, O daughter, and consider, and incline thine ear; forget also thine own people, and thy father’s house;

### Psalms 45:11 `psa45v11`

So shall the king greatly desire thy beauty: for he is thy Lord; and worship thou him.

### Psalms 45:12 `psa45v12`

And the daughter of Tyre shall be there with a gift; even the rich among the people shall intreat thy favour.

### Psalms 45:13 `psa45v13`

The king’s daughter is all glorious within: her clothing is of wrought gold.

### Psalms 45:14 `psa45v14`

She shall be brought unto the king in raiment of needlework: the virgins her companions that follow her shall be brought unto thee.

### Psalms 45:15 `psa45v15`

With gladness and rejoicing shall they be brought: they shall enter into the king’s palace.

### Psalms 45:16 `psa45v16`

Instead of thy fathers shall be thy children, whom thou mayest make princes in all the earth.

### Psalms 45:17 `psa45v17`

I will make thy name to be remembered in all generations: therefore shall the people praise thee for ever and ever.
