# Hosea 6

Verses are addressed by atomic reference token — `hos6v1` is Hosea 6:1 — because the search tokenizer splits `Hosea 6:1` into separate terms and cannot then tell it from `Hosea 1:6`. See [addressing scripture](../../conventions/addressing-scripture.md).

## KJV — Hosea 6

### Hosea 6:1 `hos6v1`

Come, and let us return unto the Lord: for he hath torn, and he will heal us; he hath smitten, and he will bind us up.

### Hosea 6:2 `hos6v2`

After two days will he revive us: in the third day he will raise us up, and we shall live in his sight.

### Hosea 6:3 `hos6v3`

Then shall we know, if we follow on to know the Lord: his going forth is prepared as the morning; and he shall come unto us as the rain, as the latter and former rain unto the earth.

### Hosea 6:4 `hos6v4`

O Ephraim, what shall I do unto thee? O Judah, what shall I do unto thee? for your goodness is as a morning cloud, and as the early dew it goeth away.

### Hosea 6:5 `hos6v5`

Therefore have I hewed them by the prophets; I have slain them by the words of my mouth: and thy judgments are as the light that goeth forth.

### Hosea 6:6 `hos6v6`

For I desired mercy, and not sacrifice; and the knowledge of God more than burnt offerings.

### Hosea 6:7 `hos6v7`

But they like men have transgressed the covenant: there have they dealt treacherously against me.

### Hosea 6:8 `hos6v8`

Gilead is a city of them that work iniquity, and is polluted with blood.

### Hosea 6:9 `hos6v9`

And as troops of robbers wait for a man, so the company of priests murder in the way by consent: for they commit lewdness.

### Hosea 6:10 `hos6v10`

I have seen an horrible thing in the house of Israel: there is the whoredom of Ephraim, Israel is defiled.

### Hosea 6:11 `hos6v11`

Also, O Judah, he hath set an harvest for thee, when I returned the captivity of my people.
