Verses are addressed by atomic reference token — `psa21v1` is Psalms 21:1 — because the search tokenizer splits `Psalms 21:1` into separate terms and cannot then tell it from `Psalms 1:21`. See [addressing scripture](../../conventions/addressing-scripture.md).

## KJV — Psalms 21

### Psalms 21:1 `psa21v1`

To the chief Musician, A Psalm of David. The king shall joy in thy strength, O Lord; and in thy salvation how greatly shall he rejoice!

### Psalms 21:2 `psa21v2`

Thou hast given him his heart’s desire, and hast not withholden the request of his lips. Selah.

### Psalms 21:3 `psa21v3`

For thou preventest him with the blessings of goodness: thou settest a crown of pure gold on his head.

### Psalms 21:4 `psa21v4`

He asked life of thee, and thou gavest it him, even length of days for ever and ever.

### Psalms 21:5 `psa21v5`

His glory is great in thy salvation: honour and majesty hast thou laid upon him.

### Psalms 21:6 `psa21v6`

For thou hast made him most blessed for ever: thou hast made him exceeding glad with thy countenance.

### Psalms 21:7 `psa21v7`

For the king trusteth in the Lord, and through the mercy of the most High he shall not be moved.

### Psalms 21:8 `psa21v8`

Thine hand shall find out all thine enemies: thy right hand shall find out those that hate thee.

### Psalms 21:9 `psa21v9`

Thou shalt make them as a fiery oven in the time of thine anger: the Lord shall swallow them up in his wrath, and the fire shall devour them.

### Psalms 21:10 `psa21v10`

Their fruit shalt thou destroy from the earth, and their seed from among the children of men.

### Psalms 21:11 `psa21v11`

For they intended evil against thee: they imagined a mischievous device, which they are not able to perform.

### Psalms 21:12 `psa21v12`

Therefore shalt thou make them turn their back, when thou shalt make ready thine arrows upon thy strings against the face of them.

### Psalms 21:13 `psa21v13`

Be thou exalted, Lord, in thine own strength: so will we sing and praise thy power.
