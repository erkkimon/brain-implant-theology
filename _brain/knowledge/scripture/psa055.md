## KJV — Psalms 55

### Psalms 55:1 `psa55v1`

To the chief Musician on Neginoth, Maschil, A Psalm of David. Give ear to my prayer, O God; and hide not thyself from my supplication.

### Psalms 55:2 `psa55v2`

Attend unto me, and hear me: I mourn in my complaint, and make a noise;

### Psalms 55:3 `psa55v3`

Because of the voice of the enemy, because of the oppression of the wicked: for they cast iniquity upon me, and in wrath they hate me.

### Psalms 55:4 `psa55v4`

My heart is sore pained within me: and the terrors of death are fallen upon me.

### Psalms 55:5 `psa55v5`

Fearfulness and trembling are come upon me, and horror hath overwhelmed me.

### Psalms 55:6 `psa55v6`

And I said, Oh that I had wings like a dove! for then would I fly away, and be at rest.

### Psalms 55:7 `psa55v7`

Lo, then would I wander far off, and remain in the wilderness. Selah.

### Psalms 55:8 `psa55v8`

I would hasten my escape from the windy storm and tempest.

### Psalms 55:9 `psa55v9`

Destroy, O Lord, and divide their tongues: for I have seen violence and strife in the city.

### Psalms 55:10 `psa55v10`

Day and night they go about it upon the walls thereof: mischief also and sorrow are in the midst of it.

### Psalms 55:11 `psa55v11`

Wickedness is in the midst thereof: deceit and guile depart not from her streets.

### Psalms 55:12 `psa55v12`

For it was not an enemy that reproached me; then I could have borne it: neither was it he that hated me that did magnify himself against me; then I would have hid myself from him:

### Psalms 55:13 `psa55v13`

But it was thou, a man mine equal, my guide, and mine acquaintance.

### Psalms 55:14 `psa55v14`

We took sweet counsel together, and walked unto the house of God in company.

### Psalms 55:15 `psa55v15`

Let death seize upon them, and let them go down quick into hell: for wickedness is in their dwellings, and among them.

### Psalms 55:16 `psa55v16`

As for me, I will call upon God; and the Lord shall save me.

### Psalms 55:17 `psa55v17`

Evening, and morning, and at noon, will I pray, and cry aloud: and he shall hear my voice.

### Psalms 55:18 `psa55v18`

He hath delivered my soul in peace from the battle that was against me: for there were many with me.

### Psalms 55:19 `psa55v19`

God shall hear, and afflict them, even he that abideth of old. Selah. Because they have no changes, therefore they fear not God.

### Psalms 55:20 `psa55v20`

He hath put forth his hands against such as be at peace with him: he hath broken his covenant.

### Psalms 55:21 `psa55v21`

The words of his mouth were smoother than butter, but war was in his heart: his words were softer than oil, yet were they drawn swords.

### Psalms 55:22 `psa55v22`

Cast thy burden upon the Lord, and he shall sustain thee: he shall never suffer the righteous to be moved.

### Psalms 55:23 `psa55v23`

But thou, O God, shalt bring them down into the pit of destruction: bloody and deceitful men shall not live out half their days; but I will trust in thee.
