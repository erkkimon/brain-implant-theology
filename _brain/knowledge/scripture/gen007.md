## KJV — Genesis 7

### Genesis 7:1 `gen7v1`

And the Lord said unto Noah, Come thou and all thy house into the ark; for thee have I seen righteous before me in this generation.

### Genesis 7:2 `gen7v2`

Of every clean beast thou shalt take to thee by sevens, the male and his female: and of beasts that are not clean by two, the male and his female.

### Genesis 7:3 `gen7v3`

Of fowls also of the air by sevens, the male and the female; to keep seed alive upon the face of all the earth.

For yet seven days, and I will cause it to rain upon the earth forty days and forty nights; and every living substance that I have made will I destroy from off the face of the earth.

### Genesis 7:5 `gen7v5`

And Noah did according unto all that the Lord commanded him.

### Genesis 7:6 `gen7v6`

And Noah was six hundred years old when the flood of waters was upon the earth.

### Genesis 7:7 `gen7v7`

### Genesis 7:9 `gen7v9`

There went in two and two unto Noah into the ark, the male and the female, as God had commanded Noah.

### Genesis 7:10 `gen7v10`

And it came to pass after seven days, that the waters of the flood were upon the earth.

### Genesis 7:11 `gen7v11`

In the six hundredth year of Noah’s life, in the second month, the seventeenth day of the month, the same day were all the fountains of the great deep broken up, and the windows of heaven were opened.

### Genesis 7:12 `gen7v12`

And the rain was upon the earth forty days and forty nights.

### Genesis 7:13 `gen7v13`

### Genesis 7:14 `gen7v14`

They, and every beast after his kind, and all the cattle after their kind, and every creeping thing that creepeth upon the earth after his kind, and every fowl after his kind, every bird of every sort.

### Genesis 7:15 `gen7v15`

And they went in unto Noah into the ark, two and two of all flesh, wherein is the breath of life.

### Genesis 7:16 `gen7v16`

### Genesis 7:17 `gen7v17`

### Genesis 7:18 `gen7v18`

And the waters prevailed, and were increased greatly upon the earth; and the ark went upon the face of the waters.

### Genesis 7:19 `gen7v19`

And the waters prevailed exceedingly upon the earth; and all the high hills, that were under the whole heaven, were covered.

### Genesis 7:20 `gen7v20`

Fifteen cubits upward did the waters prevail; and the mountains were covered.

### Genesis 7:21 `gen7v21`

### Genesis 7:22 `gen7v22`

### Genesis 7:23 `gen7v23`
