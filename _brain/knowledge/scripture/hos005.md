# Hosea 5

Verses are addressed by atomic reference token — `hos5v1` is Hosea 5:1 — because the search tokenizer splits `Hosea 5:1` into separate terms and cannot then tell it from `Hosea 1:5`. See [addressing scripture](../../conventions/addressing-scripture.md).

## KJV — Hosea 5

### Hosea 5:1 `hos5v1`

Hear ye this, O priests; and hearken, ye house of Israel; and give ye ear, O house of the king; for judgment is toward you, because ye have been a snare on Mizpah, and a net spread upon Tabor.

### Hosea 5:2 `hos5v2`

And the revolters are profound to make slaughter, though I have been a rebuker of them all.

### Hosea 5:3 `hos5v3`

I know Ephraim, and Israel is not hid from me: for now, O Ephraim, thou committest whoredom, and Israel is defiled.

### Hosea 5:4 `hos5v4`

They will not frame their doings to turn unto their God: for the spirit of whoredoms is in the midst of them, and they have not known the Lord.

### Hosea 5:5 `hos5v5`

And the pride of Israel doth testify to his face: therefore shall Israel and Ephraim fall in their iniquity; Judah also shall fall with them.

### Hosea 5:6 `hos5v6`

They shall go with their flocks and with their herds to seek the Lord; but they shall not find him; he hath withdrawn himself from them.

### Hosea 5:7 `hos5v7`

They have dealt treacherously against the Lord: for they have begotten strange children: now shall a month devour them with their portions.

### Hosea 5:8 `hos5v8`

Blow ye the cornet in Gibeah, and the trumpet in Ramah: cry aloud at Beth–aven, after thee, O Benjamin.

### Hosea 5:9 `hos5v9`

Ephraim shall be desolate in the day of rebuke: among the tribes of Israel have I made known that which shall surely be.

### Hosea 5:10 `hos5v10`

The princes of Judah were like them that remove the bound: therefore I will pour out my wrath upon them like water.

### Hosea 5:11 `hos5v11`

Ephraim is oppressed and broken in judgment, because he willingly walked after the commandment.

### Hosea 5:12 `hos5v12`

Therefore will I be unto Ephraim as a moth, and to the house of Judah as rottenness.

### Hosea 5:13 `hos5v13`

When Ephraim saw his sickness, and Judah saw his wound, then went Ephraim to the Assyrian, and sent to king Jareb: yet could he not heal you, nor cure you of your wound.

### Hosea 5:14 `hos5v14`

For I will be unto Ephraim as a lion, and as a young lion to the house of Judah: I, even I, will tear and go away; I will take away, and none shall rescue him.

### Hosea 5:15 `hos5v15`

I will go and return to my place, till they acknowledge their offence, and seek my face: in their affliction they will seek me early.
