# Genesis 10

Verses are addressed by atomic reference token — `gen10v1` is Genesis 10:1 — because the search tokenizer splits `Genesis 10:1` into separate terms and cannot then tell it from `Genesis 1:10`. See [addressing scripture](../../conventions/addressing-scripture.md).

Now these are the generations of the sons of Noah, Shem, Ham, and Japheth: and unto them were sons born after the flood.
