Verses are addressed by atomic reference token — `rev15v1` is Revelation 15:1 — because the search tokenizer splits `Revelation 15:1` into separate terms and cannot then tell it from `Revelation 1:15`. See [addressing scripture](../../conventions/addressing-scripture.md).
