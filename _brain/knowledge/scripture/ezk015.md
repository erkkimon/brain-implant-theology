# Ezekiel 15

Verses are addressed by atomic reference token — `ezk15v1` is Ezekiel 15:1 — because the search tokenizer splits `Ezekiel 15:1` into separate terms and cannot then tell it from `Ezekiel 1:15`. See [addressing scripture](../../conventions/addressing-scripture.md).

## KJV — Ezekiel 15

### Ezekiel 15:1 `ezk15v1`

And the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,

### Ezekiel 15:2 `ezk15v2`

Son of man, What is the vine tree more than any tree, or than a branch which is among the trees of the forest?

### Ezekiel 15:3 `ezk15v3`

Shall wood be taken thereof to do any work? or will men take a pin of it to hang any vessel thereon?

### Ezekiel 15:4 `ezk15v4`

Behold, it is cast into the fire for fuel; the fire devoureth both the ends of it, and the midst of it is burned. Is it meet for any work?

### Ezekiel 15:5 `ezk15v5`

Behold, when it was whole, it was meet for no work: how much less shall it be meet yet for any work, when the fire hath devoured it, and it is burned?

### Ezekiel 15:6 `ezk15v6`

Therefore thus saith the Lord God; As the vine tree among the trees of the forest, which I have given to the fire for fuel, so will I give the inhabitants of Jerusalem.

### Ezekiel 15:7 `ezk15v7`

And I will set my face against them; they shall go out from one fire, and another fire shall devour them; and ye shall know that I am the Lord, when I set my face against them.

### Ezekiel 15:8 `ezk15v8`

And I will make the land desolate, because they have committed a trespass, saith the Lord God.
