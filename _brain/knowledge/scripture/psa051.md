# Psalms 51

Verses are addressed by atomic reference token — `psa51v1` is Psalms 51:1 — because the search tokenizer splits `Psalms 51:1` into separate terms and cannot then tell it from `Psalms 1:51`. See [addressing scripture](../../conventions/addressing-scripture.md).

## KJV — Psalms 51

### Psalms 51:1 `psa51v1`

To the chief Musician, A Psalm of David, when Nathan the prophet came unto him, after he had gone in to Bath–sheba. Have mercy upon me, O God, according to thy lovingkindness: according unto the multitude of thy tender mercies blot out my transgressions.

### Psalms 51:2 `psa51v2`

Wash me throughly from mine iniquity, and cleanse me from my sin.

### Psalms 51:3 `psa51v3`

For I acknowledge my transgressions: and my sin is ever before me.

### Psalms 51:4 `psa51v4`

Against thee, thee only, have I sinned, and done this evil in thy sight: that thou mightest be justified when thou speakest, and be clear when thou judgest.

### Psalms 51:5 `psa51v5`

Behold, I was shapen in iniquity; and in sin did my mother conceive me.

### Psalms 51:6 `psa51v6`

Behold, thou desirest truth in the inward parts: and in the hidden part thou shalt make me to know wisdom.

### Psalms 51:7 `psa51v7`

Purge me with hyssop, and I shall be clean: wash me, and I shall be whiter than snow.

### Psalms 51:8 `psa51v8`

Make me to hear joy and gladness; that the bones which thou hast broken may rejoice.

### Psalms 51:9 `psa51v9`

Hide thy face from my sins, and blot out all mine iniquities.

### Psalms 51:10 `psa51v10`

Create in me a clean heart, O God; and renew a right spirit within me.

### Psalms 51:11 `psa51v11`

Cast me not away from thy presence; and take not thy holy spirit from me.

### Psalms 51:12 `psa51v12`

Restore unto me the joy of thy salvation; and uphold me with thy free spirit.

### Psalms 51:13 `psa51v13`

Then will I teach transgressors thy ways; and sinners shall be converted unto thee.

### Psalms 51:14 `psa51v14`

Deliver me from bloodguiltiness, O God, thou God of my salvation: and my tongue shall sing aloud of thy righteousness.

### Psalms 51:15 `psa51v15`

O Lord, open thou my lips; and my mouth shall shew forth thy praise.

### Psalms 51:16 `psa51v16`

For thou desirest not sacrifice; else would I give it: thou delightest not in burnt offering.

### Psalms 51:17 `psa51v17`

The sacrifices of God are a broken spirit: a broken and a contrite heart, O God, thou wilt not despise.

### Psalms 51:18 `psa51v18`

Do good in thy good pleasure unto Zion: build thou the walls of Jerusalem.

### Psalms 51:19 `psa51v19`

Then shalt thou be pleased with the sacrifices of righteousness, with burnt offering and whole burnt offering: then shall they offer bullocks upon thine altar.
