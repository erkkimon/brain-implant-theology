## KJV — 1 Corinthians 12

### 1 Corinthians 12:1 `1co12v1`

Now concerning spiritual gifts, brethren, I would not have you ignorant.

### 1 Corinthians 12:2 `1co12v2`

Ye know that ye were Gentiles, carried away unto these dumb idols, even as ye were led.

### 1 Corinthians 12:3 `1co12v3`

Wherefore I give you to understand, that no man speaking by the Spirit of God calleth Jesus accursed: and that no man can say that Jesus is the Lord, but by the Holy Ghost.

### 1 Corinthians 12:4 `1co12v4`

Now there are diversities of gifts, but the same Spirit.

### 1 Corinthians 12:5 `1co12v5`

And there are differences of administrations, but the same Lord.

### 1 Corinthians 12:6 `1co12v6`

And there are diversities of operations, but it is the same God which worketh all in all.

### 1 Corinthians 12:7 `1co12v7`

But the manifestation of the Spirit is given to every man to profit withal.

### 1 Corinthians 12:8 `1co12v8`

For to one is given by the Spirit the word of wisdom; to another the word of knowledge by the same Spirit;

### 1 Corinthians 12:9 `1co12v9`

To another faith by the same Spirit; to another the gifts of healing by the same Spirit;

### 1 Corinthians 12:10 `1co12v10`

To another the working of miracles; to another prophecy; to another discerning of spirits; to another divers kinds of tongues; to another the interpretation of tongues:

### 1 Corinthians 12:11 `1co12v11`

But all these worketh that one and the selfsame Spirit, dividing to every man severally as he will.

### 1 Corinthians 12:12 `1co12v12`

For as the body is one, and hath many members, and all the members of that one body, being many, are one body: so also is Christ.

### 1 Corinthians 12:13 `1co12v13`

For by one Spirit are we all baptized into one body, whether we be Jews or Gentiles, whether we be bond or free; and have been all made to drink into one Spirit.

### 1 Corinthians 12:14 `1co12v14`

For the body is not one member, but many.

### 1 Corinthians 12:15 `1co12v15`

If the foot shall say, Because I am not the hand, I am not of the body; is it therefore not of the body?

### 1 Corinthians 12:16 `1co12v16`

And if the ear shall say, Because I am not the eye, I am not of the body; is it therefore not of the body?

### 1 Corinthians 12:17 `1co12v17`

If the whole body were an eye, where were the hearing? If the whole were hearing, where were the smelling?

### 1 Corinthians 12:18 `1co12v18`

But now hath God set the members every one of them in the body, as it hath pleased him.

### 1 Corinthians 12:19 `1co12v19`

And if they were all one member, where were the body?

### 1 Corinthians 12:20 `1co12v20`

But now are they many members, yet but one body.

### 1 Corinthians 12:21 `1co12v21`

And the eye cannot say unto the hand, I have no need of thee: nor again the head to the feet, I have no need of you.

### 1 Corinthians 12:22 `1co12v22`

Nay, much more those members of the body, which seem to be more feeble, are necessary:

### 1 Corinthians 12:23 `1co12v23`

And those members of the body, which we think to be less honourable, upon these we bestow more abundant honour; and our uncomely parts have more abundant comeliness.

### 1 Corinthians 12:24 `1co12v24`

For our comely parts have no need: but God hath tempered the body together, having given more abundant honour to that part which lacked:

### 1 Corinthians 12:25 `1co12v25`

That there should be no schism in the body; but that the members should have the same care one for another.

### 1 Corinthians 12:26 `1co12v26`

And whether one member suffer, all the members suffer with it; or one member be honoured, all the members rejoice with it.

### 1 Corinthians 12:27 `1co12v27`

Now ye are the body of Christ, and members in particular.

### 1 Corinthians 12:28 `1co12v28`

And God hath set some in the church, first apostles, secondarily prophets, thirdly teachers, after that miracles, then gifts of healings, helps, governments, diversities of tongues.

### 1 Corinthians 12:29 `1co12v29`

Are all apostles? are all prophets? are all teachers? are all workers of miracles?

### 1 Corinthians 12:30 `1co12v30`

Have all the gifts of healing? do all speak with tongues? do all interpret?

### 1 Corinthians 12:31 `1co12v31`

But covet earnestly the best gifts: and yet shew I unto you a more excellent way.
