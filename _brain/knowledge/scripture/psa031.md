# Psalms 31

Verses are addressed by atomic reference token — `psa31v1` is Psalms 31:1 — because the search tokenizer splits `Psalms 31:1` into separate terms and cannot then tell it from `Psalms 1:31`. See [addressing scripture](../../conventions/addressing-scripture.md).

## KJV — Psalms 31

### Psalms 31:1 `psa31v1`

To the chief Musician, A Psalm of David. In thee, O Lord, do I put my trust; let me never be ashamed: deliver me in thy righteousness.

### Psalms 31:2 `psa31v2`

Bow down thine ear to me; deliver me speedily: be thou my strong rock, for an house of defence to save me.

### Psalms 31:3 `psa31v3`

For thou art my rock and my fortress; therefore for thy name’s sake lead me, and guide me.

### Psalms 31:4 `psa31v4`

Pull me out of the net that they have laid privily for me: for thou art my strength.

### Psalms 31:5 `psa31v5`

Into thine hand I commit my spirit: thou hast redeemed me, O Lord God of truth.

### Psalms 31:6 `psa31v6`

I have hated them that regard lying vanities: but I trust in the Lord.

### Psalms 31:7 `psa31v7`

I will be glad and rejoice in thy mercy: for thou hast considered my trouble; thou hast known my soul in adversities;

### Psalms 31:8 `psa31v8`

And hast not shut me up into the hand of the enemy: thou hast set my feet in a large room.

### Psalms 31:9 `psa31v9`

Have mercy upon me, O Lord, for I am in trouble: mine eye is consumed with grief, yea, my soul and my belly.

### Psalms 31:10 `psa31v10`

For my life is spent with grief, and my years with sighing: my strength faileth because of mine iniquity, and my bones are consumed.

### Psalms 31:11 `psa31v11`

I was a reproach among all mine enemies, but especially among my neighbours, and a fear to mine acquaintance: they that did see me without fled from me.

### Psalms 31:12 `psa31v12`

I am forgotten as a dead man out of mind: I am like a broken vessel.

### Psalms 31:13 `psa31v13`

For I have heard the slander of many: fear was on every side: while they took counsel together against me, they devised to take away my life.

### Psalms 31:14 `psa31v14`

But I trusted in thee, O Lord: I said, Thou art my God.

### Psalms 31:15 `psa31v15`

My times are in thy hand: deliver me from the hand of mine enemies, and from them that persecute me.

### Psalms 31:16 `psa31v16`

Make thy face to shine upon thy servant: save me for thy mercies’ sake.

### Psalms 31:17 `psa31v17`

Let me not be ashamed, O Lord; for I have called upon thee: let the wicked be ashamed, and let them be silent in the grave.

### Psalms 31:18 `psa31v18`

Let the lying lips be put to silence; which speak grievous things proudly and contemptuously against the righteous.

### Psalms 31:19 `psa31v19`

Oh how great is thy goodness, which thou hast laid up for them that fear thee; which thou hast wrought for them that trust in thee before the sons of men!

### Psalms 31:20 `psa31v20`

Thou shalt hide them in the secret of thy presence from the pride of man: thou shalt keep them secretly in a pavilion from the strife of tongues.

### Psalms 31:21 `psa31v21`

Blessed be the Lord: for he hath shewed me his marvellous kindness in a strong city.

### Psalms 31:22 `psa31v22`

For I said in my haste, I am cut off from before thine eyes: nevertheless thou heardest the voice of my supplications when I cried unto thee.

### Psalms 31:23 `psa31v23`

O love the Lord, all ye his saints: for the Lord preserveth the faithful, and plentifully rewardeth the proud doer.

### Psalms 31:24 `psa31v24`

Be of good courage, and he shall strengthen your heart, all ye that hope in the Lord.
