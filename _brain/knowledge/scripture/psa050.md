## KJV — Psalms 50

### Psalms 50:1 `psa50v1`

A Psalm of Asaph. The mighty God, even the Lord, hath spoken, and called the earth from the rising of the sun unto the going down thereof.

### Psalms 50:2 `psa50v2`

Out of Zion, the perfection of beauty, God hath shined.

### Psalms 50:3 `psa50v3`

Our God shall come, and shall not keep silence: a fire shall devour before him, and it shall be very tempestuous round about him.

### Psalms 50:4 `psa50v4`

He shall call to the heavens from above, and to the earth, that he may judge his people.

### Psalms 50:5 `psa50v5`

Gather my saints together unto me; those that have made a covenant with me by sacrifice.

### Psalms 50:6 `psa50v6`

And the heavens shall declare his righteousness: for God is judge himself. Selah.

### Psalms 50:7 `psa50v7`

Hear, O my people, and I will speak; O Israel, and I will testify against thee: I am God, even thy God.

### Psalms 50:8 `psa50v8`

I will not reprove thee for thy sacrifices or thy burnt offerings, to have been continually before me.

### Psalms 50:9 `psa50v9`

I will take no bullock out of thy house, nor he goats out of thy folds.

### Psalms 50:10 `psa50v10`

For every beast of the forest is mine, and the cattle upon a thousand hills.

### Psalms 50:11 `psa50v11`

I know all the fowls of the mountains: and the wild beasts of the field are mine.

### Psalms 50:12 `psa50v12`

If I were hungry, I would not tell thee: for the world is mine, and the fulness thereof.

### Psalms 50:13 `psa50v13`

Will I eat the flesh of bulls, or drink the blood of goats?

### Psalms 50:14 `psa50v14`

Offer unto God thanksgiving; and pay thy vows unto the most High:

### Psalms 50:15 `psa50v15`

And call upon me in the day of trouble: I will deliver thee, and thou shalt glorify me.

### Psalms 50:16 `psa50v16`

But unto the wicked God saith, What hast thou to do to declare my statutes, or that thou shouldest take my covenant in thy mouth?

### Psalms 50:17 `psa50v17`

Seeing thou hatest instruction, and castest my words behind thee.

### Psalms 50:18 `psa50v18`

When thou sawest a thief, then thou consentedst with him, and hast been partaker with adulterers.

### Psalms 50:19 `psa50v19`

Thou givest thy mouth to evil, and thy tongue frameth deceit.

### Psalms 50:20 `psa50v20`

Thou sittest and speakest against thy brother; thou slanderest thine own mother’s son.

### Psalms 50:21 `psa50v21`

These things hast thou done, and I kept silence; thou thoughtest that I was altogether such an one as thyself: but I will reprove thee, and set them in order before thine eyes.

### Psalms 50:22 `psa50v22`

Now consider this, ye that forget God, lest I tear you in pieces, and there be none to deliver.

### Psalms 50:23 `psa50v23`

Whoso offereth praise glorifieth me: and to him that ordereth his conversation aright will I shew the salvation of God.
