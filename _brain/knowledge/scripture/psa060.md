Verses are addressed by atomic reference token — `psa60v1` is Psalms 60:1 — because the search tokenizer splits `Psalms 60:1` into separate terms and cannot then tell it from `Psalms 1:60`. See [addressing scripture](../../conventions/addressing-scripture.md).

## KJV — Psalms 60

### Psalms 60:1 `psa60v1`

To the chief Musician upon Shushan–eduth, Michtam of David, to teach; when he strove with Aram–naharaim and with Aram–zobah, when Joab returned, and smote of Edom in the valley of salt twelve thousand. O God, thou hast cast us off, thou hast scattered us, thou hast been displeased; O turn thyself to us again.

### Psalms 60:2 `psa60v2`

Thou hast made the earth to tremble; thou hast broken it: heal the breaches thereof; for it shaketh.

### Psalms 60:3 `psa60v3`

Thou hast shewed thy people hard things: thou hast made us to drink the wine of astonishment.

### Psalms 60:4 `psa60v4`

Thou hast given a banner to them that fear thee, that it may be displayed because of the truth. Selah.

### Psalms 60:5 `psa60v5`

That thy beloved may be delivered; save with thy right hand, and hear me.

### Psalms 60:6 `psa60v6`

God hath spoken in his holiness; I will rejoice, I will divide Shechem, and mete out the valley of Succoth.

### Psalms 60:7 `psa60v7`

Gilead is mine, and Manasseh is mine; Ephraim also is the strength of mine head; Judah is my lawgiver;

### Psalms 60:8 `psa60v8`

Moab is my washpot; over Edom will I cast out my shoe: Philistia, triumph thou because of me.

### Psalms 60:9 `psa60v9`

Who will bring me into the strong city? who will lead me into Edom?

### Psalms 60:10 `psa60v10`

Wilt not thou, O God, which hadst cast us off? and thou, O God, which didst not go out with our armies?

### Psalms 60:11 `psa60v11`

Give us help from trouble: for vain is the help of man.

### Psalms 60:12 `psa60v12`

Through God we shall do valiantly: for he it is that shall tread down our enemies.
