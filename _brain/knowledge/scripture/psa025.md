## KJV — Psalms 25

### Psalms 25:1 `psa25v1`

A Psalm of David. Unto thee, O Lord, do I lift up my soul.

### Psalms 25:2 `psa25v2`

O my God, I trust in thee: let me not be ashamed, let not mine enemies triumph over me.

### Psalms 25:3 `psa25v3`

Yea, let none that wait on thee be ashamed: let them be ashamed which transgress without cause.

### Psalms 25:4 `psa25v4`

Shew me thy ways, O Lord; teach me thy paths.

### Psalms 25:5 `psa25v5`

Lead me in thy truth, and teach me: for thou art the God of my salvation; on thee do I wait all the day.

### Psalms 25:6 `psa25v6`

Remember, O Lord, thy tender mercies and thy lovingkindnesses; for they have been ever of old.

### Psalms 25:7 `psa25v7`

Remember not the sins of my youth, nor my transgressions: according to thy mercy remember thou me for thy goodness’ sake, O Lord.

### Psalms 25:8 `psa25v8`

Good and upright is the Lord: therefore will he teach sinners in the way.

### Psalms 25:9 `psa25v9`

The meek will he guide in judgment: and the meek will he teach his way.

### Psalms 25:10 `psa25v10`

All the paths of the Lord are mercy and truth unto such as keep his covenant and his testimonies.

### Psalms 25:11 `psa25v11`

For thy name’s sake, O Lord, pardon mine iniquity; for it is great.

### Psalms 25:12 `psa25v12`

What man is he that feareth the Lord? him shall he teach in the way that he shall choose.

### Psalms 25:13 `psa25v13`

His soul shall dwell at ease; and his seed shall inherit the earth.

### Psalms 25:14 `psa25v14`

The secret of the Lord is with them that fear him; and he will shew them his covenant.

### Psalms 25:15 `psa25v15`

Mine eyes are ever toward the Lord; for he shall pluck my feet out of the net.

### Psalms 25:16 `psa25v16`

Turn thee unto me, and have mercy upon me; for I am desolate and afflicted.

### Psalms 25:17 `psa25v17`

The troubles of my heart are enlarged: O bring thou me out of my distresses.

### Psalms 25:18 `psa25v18`

Look upon mine affliction and my pain; and forgive all my sins.

### Psalms 25:19 `psa25v19`

Consider mine enemies; for they are many; and they hate me with cruel hatred.

### Psalms 25:20 `psa25v20`

O keep my soul, and deliver me: let me not be ashamed; for I put my trust in thee.

### Psalms 25:21 `psa25v21`

Let integrity and uprightness preserve me; for I wait on thee.

### Psalms 25:22 `psa25v22`

Redeem Israel, O God, out of all his troubles.
