# Old Testament

The thirty-nine books of the Hebrew Bible, from creation to the prophets — the scripture Jesus and the apostles read, cite, and fulfil.

## Books

* [Genesis](gen/gen.md) — Creation, the flood, the patriarchs, and the origin of Israel.
* [Exodus](exo/exo.md) — The exodus from Egypt, the covenant at Sinai, and the tabernacle.
* [Leviticus](lev/lev.md) — Priestly law: sacrifices, purity, and the holiness code.
* [Numbers](num/num.md) — The wilderness wandering and the census of Israel.
* [Deuteronomy](deu/deu.md) — Moses' final addresses and the renewal of the covenant.
* [Joshua](jos/jos.md) — The conquest and division of Canaan under Joshua.
* [Judges](jdg/jdg.md) — Israel's cycle of apostasy and deliverance before the monarchy.
* [Ruth](rut/rut.md) — A Moabite widow's loyalty and her place in David's lineage.
* [1 Samuel](1sa/1sa.md) — From the judges to Saul: the rise and fall of Israel's first king.
* [2 Samuel](2sa/2sa.md) — David's reign: triumph, sin, and its consequences.
* [1 Kings](1ki/1ki.md) — Solomon's kingdom, the temple, and the divided monarchy.
* [2 Kings](2ki/2ki.md) — The divided kingdoms to the Assyrian and Babylonian exiles.
* [1 Chronicles](1ch/1ch.md) — Genealogies and Saul's death, setting up David's reign.
* [2 Chronicles](2ch/2ch.md) — Solomon's temple to the Babylonian exile, from the priestly view.
* [Ezra](ezr/ezr.md) — The return from exile and the rebuilding of the temple.
* [Nehemiah](neh/neh.md) — Rebuilding Jerusalem's walls and covenant renewal under Nehemiah.
* [Esther](est/est.md) — A Jewish queen saves her people in the Persian court.
* [Job](job/job.md) — The suffering of a righteous man and the question of divine justice.
* [Psalms](psa/psa.md) — One hundred and fifty songs of praise, lament, and royal trust.
* [Proverbs](pro/pro.md) — Solomonic aphorisms on the way of wisdom and folly.
* [Ecclesiastes](ecc/ecc.md) — A philosopher's meditation on meaning under the sun.
* [Song of Solomon](sng/sng.md) — A love poem between a shepherd and a king's daughter.
* [Isaiah](isa/isa.md) — Judgement, comfort, and the servant songs over sixty-six chapters.
* [Jeremiah](jer/jer.md) — A prophet's forty-year warning before Jerusalem falls.
* [Lamentations](lam/lam.md) — Five laments over the destruction of Jerusalem.
* [Ezekiel](ezk/ezk.md) — Exilic visions: judgement, the valley of dry bones, and a new temple.
* [Daniel](dan/dan.md) — A courtier's faith under empire and apocalyptic visions.
* [Hosea](hos/hos.md) — A prophet's marriage as a parable of God's covenant with Israel.
* [Joel](jol/jol.md) — Locusts, the day of the Lord, and the outpoured Spirit.
* [Amos](amo/amo.md) — A shepherd's indictment of Israel's social injustice.
* [Obadiah](oba/oba.md) — A single chapter against Edom's pride and cruelty.
* [Jonah](jon/jon.md) — A reluctant prophet and God's mercy to Nineveh.
* [Micah](mic/mic.md) — What the Lord requires, and a ruler from Bethlehem.
* [Nahum](nam/nam.md) — The fall of Nineveh foretold in poetry.
* [Habakkuk](hab/hab.md) — The just shall live by faith, and judgement on the proud.
* [Zephaniah](zep/zep.md) — The day of the Lord and a remnant saved.
* [Haggai](hag/hag.md) — Rebuild the temple: God's house before your own houses.
* [Zechariah](zec/zec.md) — Night visions, two olive branches, and a coming king.
* [Malachi](mal/mal.md) — The last prophet before silence: robbing God, and a messenger to come.
