# Isaiah 33

Verses are addressed by atomic reference token — `isa33v1` is Isaiah 33:1 — because the search tokenizer splits `Isaiah 33:1` into separate terms and cannot then tell it from `Isaiah 1:33`. See [addressing scripture](../../conventions/addressing-scripture.md).

## KJV — Isaiah 33

### Isaiah 33:1 `isa33v1`

Woe to thee that spoilest, and thou wast not spoiled; and dealest treacherously, and they dealt not treacherously with thee! when thou shalt cease to spoil, thou shalt be spoiled; and when thou shalt make an end to deal treacherously, they shall deal treacherously with thee.

### Isaiah 33:2 `isa33v2`

O Lord, be gracious unto us; we have waited for thee: be thou their arm every morning, our salvation also in the time of trouble.

### Isaiah 33:3 `isa33v3`

At the noise of the tumult the people fled; at the lifting up of thyself the nations were scattered.

### Isaiah 33:4 `isa33v4`

And your spoil shall be gathered like the gathering of the caterpiller: as the running to and fro of locusts shall he run upon them.

### Isaiah 33:5 `isa33v5`

The Lord is exalted; for he dwelleth on high: he hath filled Zion with judgment and righteousness.

### Isaiah 33:6 `isa33v6`

And wisdom and knowledge shall be the stability of thy times, and strength of salvation: the fear of the Lord is his treasure.

### Isaiah 33:7 `isa33v7`

Behold, their valiant ones shall cry without: the ambassadors of peace shall weep bitterly.

### Isaiah 33:8 `isa33v8`

The highways lie waste, the wayfaring man ceaseth: he hath broken the covenant, he hath despised the cities, he regardeth no man.

### Isaiah 33:9 `isa33v9`

The earth mourneth and languisheth: Lebanon is ashamed and hewn down: Sharon is like a wilderness; and Bashan and Carmel shake off their fruits.

### Isaiah 33:10 `isa33v10`

Now will I rise, saith the Lord; now will I be exalted; now will I lift up myself.

### Isaiah 33:11 `isa33v11`

Ye shall conceive chaff, ye shall bring forth stubble: your breath, as fire, shall devour you.

### Isaiah 33:12 `isa33v12`

And the people shall be as the burnings of lime: as thorns cut up shall they be burned in the fire.

### Isaiah 33:13 `isa33v13`

Hear, ye that are far off, what I have done; and, ye that are near, acknowledge my might.

### Isaiah 33:14 `isa33v14`

The sinners in Zion are afraid; fearfulness hath surprised the hypocrites. Who among us shall dwell with the devouring fire? who among us shall dwell with everlasting burnings?

### Isaiah 33:15 `isa33v15`

He that walketh righteously, and speaketh uprightly; he that despiseth the gain of oppressions, that shaketh his hands from holding of bribes, that stoppeth his ears from hearing of blood, and shutteth his eyes from seeing evil;

### Isaiah 33:16 `isa33v16`

He shall dwell on high: his place of defence shall be the munitions of rocks: bread shall be given him; his waters shall be sure.

### Isaiah 33:17 `isa33v17`

Thine eyes shall see the king in his beauty: they shall behold the land that is very far off.

### Isaiah 33:18 `isa33v18`

Thine heart shall meditate terror. Where is the scribe? where is the receiver? where is he that counted the towers?

### Isaiah 33:19 `isa33v19`

Thou shalt not see a fierce people, a people of a deeper speech than thou canst perceive; of a stammering tongue, that thou canst not understand.

### Isaiah 33:20 `isa33v20`

Look upon Zion, the city of our solemnities: thine eyes shall see Jerusalem a quiet habitation, a tabernacle that shall not be taken down; not one of the stakes thereof shall ever be removed, neither shall any of the cords thereof be broken.

### Isaiah 33:21 `isa33v21`

But there the glorious Lord will be unto us a place of broad rivers and streams; wherein shall go no galley with oars, neither shall gallant ship pass thereby.

### Isaiah 33:22 `isa33v22`

For the Lord is our judge, the Lord is our lawgiver, the Lord is our king; he will save us.

### Isaiah 33:23 `isa33v23`

Thy tacklings are loosed; they could not well strengthen their mast, they could not spread the sail: then is the prey of a great spoil divided; the lame take the prey.

### Isaiah 33:24 `isa33v24`

And the inhabitant shall not say, I am sick: the people that dwell therein shall be forgiven their iniquity.
