### Proverbs 18:1 `pro18v1`

### Proverbs 18:2 `pro18v2`

### Proverbs 18:3 `pro18v3`

The words of a man’s mouth are as deep waters, and the wellspring of wisdom as a flowing brook.

### Proverbs 18:5 `pro18v5`

It is not good to accept the person of the wicked, to overthrow the righteous in judgment.

### Proverbs 18:6 `pro18v6`

### Proverbs 18:9 `pro18v9`

### Proverbs 18:10 `pro18v10`

The name of the Lord is a strong tower: the righteous runneth into it, and is safe.

### Proverbs 18:11 `pro18v11`

### Proverbs 18:12 `pro18v12`

### Proverbs 18:14 `pro18v14`

### Proverbs 18:15 `pro18v15`

### Proverbs 18:16 `pro18v16`

### Proverbs 18:18 `pro18v18`

### Proverbs 18:21 `pro18v21`

### Proverbs 18:22 `pro18v22`

### Proverbs 18:23 `pro18v23`
