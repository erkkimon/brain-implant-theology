## KJV — Proverbs 23

### Proverbs 23:1 `pro23v1`

When thou sittest to eat with a ruler, consider diligently what is before thee:

### Proverbs 23:2 `pro23v2`

And put a knife to thy throat, if thou be a man given to appetite.

### Proverbs 23:3 `pro23v3`

Be not desirous of his dainties: for they are deceitful meat.

### Proverbs 23:4 `pro23v4`

Labour not to be rich: cease from thine own wisdom.

### Proverbs 23:5 `pro23v5`

Wilt thou set thine eyes upon that which is not? for riches certainly make themselves wings; they fly away as an eagle toward heaven.

### Proverbs 23:6 `pro23v6`

Eat thou not the bread of him that hath an evil eye, neither desire thou his dainty meats:

### Proverbs 23:7 `pro23v7`

For as he thinketh in his heart, so is he: Eat and drink, saith he to thee; but his heart is not with thee.

### Proverbs 23:8 `pro23v8`

The morsel which thou hast eaten shalt thou vomit up, and lose thy sweet words.

### Proverbs 23:9 `pro23v9`

Speak not in the ears of a fool: for he will despise the wisdom of thy words.

### Proverbs 23:10 `pro23v10`

Remove not the old landmark; and enter not into the fields of the fatherless:

### Proverbs 23:11 `pro23v11`

For their redeemer is mighty; he shall plead their cause with thee.

### Proverbs 23:12 `pro23v12`

Apply thine heart unto instruction, and thine ears to the words of knowledge.

### Proverbs 23:13 `pro23v13`

Withhold not correction from the child: for if thou beatest him with the rod, he shall not die.

### Proverbs 23:14 `pro23v14`

Thou shalt beat him with the rod, and shalt deliver his soul from hell.

### Proverbs 23:15 `pro23v15`

My son, if thine heart be wise, my heart shall rejoice, even mine.

### Proverbs 23:16 `pro23v16`

Yea, my reins shall rejoice, when thy lips speak right things.

### Proverbs 23:17 `pro23v17`

Let not thine heart envy sinners: but be thou in the fear of the Lord all the day long.

### Proverbs 23:18 `pro23v18`

For surely there is an end; and thine expectation shall not be cut off.

### Proverbs 23:19 `pro23v19`

Hear thou, my son, and be wise, and guide thine heart in the way.

### Proverbs 23:20 `pro23v20`

Be not among winebibbers; among riotous eaters of flesh:

### Proverbs 23:21 `pro23v21`

For the drunkard and the glutton shall come to poverty: and drowsiness shall clothe a man with rags.

### Proverbs 23:22 `pro23v22`

Hearken unto thy father that begat thee, and despise not thy mother when she is old.

### Proverbs 23:23 `pro23v23`

Buy the truth, and sell it not; also wisdom, and instruction, and understanding.

### Proverbs 23:24 `pro23v24`

The father of the righteous shall greatly rejoice: and he that begetteth a wise child shall have joy of him.

### Proverbs 23:25 `pro23v25`

Thy father and thy mother shall be glad, and she that bare thee shall rejoice.

### Proverbs 23:26 `pro23v26`

My son, give me thine heart, and let thine eyes observe my ways.

### Proverbs 23:27 `pro23v27`

For a whore is a deep ditch; and a strange woman is a narrow pit.

### Proverbs 23:28 `pro23v28`

She also lieth in wait as for a prey, and increaseth the transgressors among men.

### Proverbs 23:29 `pro23v29`

Who hath woe? who hath sorrow? who hath contentions? who hath babbling? who hath wounds without cause? who hath redness of eyes?

### Proverbs 23:30 `pro23v30`

They that tarry long at the wine; they that go to seek mixed wine.

### Proverbs 23:31 `pro23v31`

Look not thou upon the wine when it is red, when it giveth his colour in the cup, when it moveth itself aright.

### Proverbs 23:32 `pro23v32`

At the last it biteth like a serpent, and stingeth like an adder.

### Proverbs 23:33 `pro23v33`

Thine eyes shall behold strange women, and thine heart shall utter perverse things.

### Proverbs 23:34 `pro23v34`

Yea, thou shalt be as he that lieth down in the midst of the sea, or as he that lieth upon the top of a mast.

### Proverbs 23:35 `pro23v35`
