Verses are addressed by atomic reference token — `psa70v1` is Psalms 70:1 — because the search tokenizer splits `Psalms 70:1` into separate terms and cannot then tell it from `Psalms 1:70`. See [addressing scripture](../../conventions/addressing-scripture.md).

## KJV — Psalms 70

### Psalms 70:1 `psa70v1`

To the chief Musician, A Psalm of David, to bring to remembrance. Make haste, O God, to deliver me; make haste to help me, O Lord.

### Psalms 70:2 `psa70v2`

Let them be ashamed and confounded that seek after my soul: let them be turned backward, and put to confusion, that desire my hurt.

### Psalms 70:3 `psa70v3`

Let them be turned back for a reward of their shame that say, Aha, aha.

### Psalms 70:4 `psa70v4`

Let all those that seek thee rejoice and be glad in thee: and let such as love thy salvation say continually, Let God be magnified.

### Psalms 70:5 `psa70v5`

But I am poor and needy: make haste unto me, O God: thou art my help and my deliverer; O Lord, make no tarrying.
