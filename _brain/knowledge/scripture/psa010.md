# Psalms 10

Part of [Psalms 10](psa/psa.md).

Verses are addressed by atomic reference token — `psa10v1` is Psalms 10:1 — because the search tokenizer splits `Psalms 10:1` into separate terms and cannot then tell it from `Psalms 1:10`. See [addressing scripture](../../conventions/addressing-scripture.md).

## KJV — Psalms 10

### Psalms 10:1 `psa10v1`

Why standest thou afar off, O Lord? why hidest thou thyself in times of trouble?

### Psalms 10:2 `psa10v2`

The wicked in his pride doth persecute the poor: let them be taken in the devices that they have imagined.

### Psalms 10:3 `psa10v3`

For the wicked boasteth of his heart’s desire, and blesseth the covetous, whom the Lord abhorreth.

### Psalms 10:4 `psa10v4`

The wicked, through the pride of his countenance, will not seek after God: God is not in all his thoughts.

### Psalms 10:5 `psa10v5`

His ways are always grievous; thy judgments are far above out of his sight: as for all his enemies, he puffeth at them.

### Psalms 10:6 `psa10v6`

He hath said in his heart, I shall not be moved: for I shall never be in adversity.

### Psalms 10:7 `psa10v7`

His mouth is full of cursing and deceit and fraud: under his tongue is mischief and vanity.

### Psalms 10:8 `psa10v8`

He sitteth in the lurking places of the villages: in the secret places doth he murder the innocent: his eyes are privily set against the poor.

### Psalms 10:9 `psa10v9`

He lieth in wait secretly as a lion in his den: he lieth in wait to catch the poor: he doth catch the poor, when he draweth him into his net.

### Psalms 10:10 `psa10v10`

He croucheth, and humbleth himself, that the poor may fall by his strong ones.

### Psalms 10:11 `psa10v11`

He hath said in his heart, God hath forgotten: he hideth his face; he will never see it.

### Psalms 10:12 `psa10v12`

Arise, O Lord; O God, lift up thine hand: forget not the humble.

### Psalms 10:13 `psa10v13`

Wherefore doth the wicked contemn God? he hath said in his heart, Thou wilt not require it.

### Psalms 10:14 `psa10v14`

Thou hast seen it; for thou beholdest mischief and spite, to requite it with thy hand: the poor committeth himself unto thee; thou art the helper of the fatherless.

### Psalms 10:15 `psa10v15`

Break thou the arm of the wicked and the evil man: seek out his wickedness till thou find none.

### Psalms 10:16 `psa10v16`

The Lord is King for ever and ever: the heathen are perished out of his land.

### Psalms 10:17 `psa10v17`

Lord, thou hast heard the desire of the humble: thou wilt prepare their heart, thou wilt cause thine ear to hear:

### Psalms 10:18 `psa10v18`

To judge the fatherless and the oppressed, that the man of the earth may no more oppress.
