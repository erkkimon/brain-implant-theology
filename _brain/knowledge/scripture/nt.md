# New Testament

The twenty-seven books of the apostolic witness: four gospels, the church's birth, letters to churches and pastors, and the apocalypse.

## Books

* [Matthew](mat/mat.md) — Jesus as the promised king: genealogy, Sermon on the Mount, passion.
* [Mark](mrk/mrk.md) — The shortest gospel: Jesus acts with authority and suffers as servant.
* [Luke](luk/luk.md) — A historian's account: Jesus among the lost, the poor, and the marginal.
* [John](jhn/jhn.md) — Signs and discourses: Jesus as the Word made flesh and the way, truth, life.
* [Acts](act/act.md) — The Spirit given, the church born, and the gospel reaching Rome.
* [Romans](rom/rom.md) — Justification by faith, life in the Spirit, and God's plan for Israel.
* [1 Corinthians](1co/1co.md) — A divided church corrected: gifts, the body, love, and the resurrection.
* [2 Corinthians](2co/2co.md) — Paul's defence of his apostleship and the generosity of the new covenant.
* [Galatians](gal/gal.md) — Justification by faith alone; no return to the law.
* [Ephesians](eph/eph.md) — The cosmic scope of the church: one body, one Spirit, one new humanity.
* [Philippians](php/php.md) — Joy in chains: Christ's mind, the gospel's advance, and contentment.
* [Colossians](col/col.md) — Christ as the image of the invisible God, head of all things.
* [1 Thessalonians](1th/1th.md) — Comfort in bereavement and the hope of the Lord's return.
* [2 Thessalonians](2th/2th.md) — The man of lawlessness and the stability of the believer.
* [1 Timothy](1ti/1ti.md) — Church order, elders and deacons, and the good fight of faith.
* [2 Timothy](2ti/2ti.md) — Paul's last letter: guard the deposit, preach the word, endure.
* [Titus](tit/tit.md) — Set in order what was left: elders, sound doctrine, and good works.
* [Philemon](phm/phm.md) — A runaway slave received as a brother: the gospel's social reach.
* [Hebrews](heb/heb.md) — Christ greater than angels, Moses, and the old covenant's priesthood.
* [James](jas/jas.md) — Faith without works is dead: practical wisdom for the diaspora.
* [1 Peter](1pe/1pe.md) — Suffering as a Christian and a holy identity under fire.
* [2 Peter](2pe/2pe.md) — False teachers, the certainty of Christ's return, and growth in grace.
* [1 John](1jn/1jn.md) — Light, love, and the test of true fellowship with God.
* [2 John](2jn/2jn.md) — Walking in truth and love, and refusing those who deny Christ's flesh.
* [3 John](3jn/3jn.md) — Hospitality commended, and a self-serving leader named.
* [Jude](jud/jud.md) — Contend for the faith against those who turn grace into licence.
* [Revelation](rev/rev.md) — Seven churches, a heavenly throne, and the Lamb's victory over the beast.
