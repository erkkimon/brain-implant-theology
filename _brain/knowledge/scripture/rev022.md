Verses are addressed by atomic reference token — `rev22v1` is Revelation 22:1 — because the search tokenizer splits `Revelation 22:1` into separate terms and cannot then tell it from `Revelation 1:22`. See [addressing scripture](../../conventions/addressing-scripture.md).
