# Micah 6

Verses are addressed by atomic reference token — `mic6v1` is Micah 6:1 — because the search tokenizer splits `Micah 6:1` into separate terms and cannot then tell it from `Micah 1:6`. See [addressing scripture](../../conventions/addressing-scripture.md).

## KJV — Micah 6

### Micah 6:1 `mic6v1`

Hear ye now what the Lord saith; Arise, contend thou before the mountains, and let the hills hear thy voice.

### Micah 6:2 `mic6v2`

Hear ye, O mountains, the Lord’s controversy, and ye strong foundations of the earth: for the Lord hath a controversy with his people, and he will plead with Israel.

### Micah 6:3 `mic6v3`

O my people, what have I done unto thee? and wherein have I wearied thee? testify against me.

### Micah 6:4 `mic6v4`

For I brought thee up out of the land of Egypt, and redeemed thee out of the house of servants; and I sent before thee Moses, Aaron, and Miriam.

### Micah 6:5 `mic6v5`

O my people, remember now what Balak king of Moab consulted, and what Balaam the son of Beor answered him from Shittim unto Gilgal; that ye may know the righteousness of the Lord.

### Micah 6:6 `mic6v6`

Wherewith shall I come before the Lord, and bow myself before the high God? shall I come before him with burnt offerings, with calves of a year old?

### Micah 6:7 `mic6v7`

Will the Lord be pleased with thousands of rams, or with ten thousands of rivers of oil? shall I give my firstborn for my transgression, the fruit of my body for the sin of my soul?

### Micah 6:8 `mic6v8`

He hath shewed thee, O man, what is good; and what doth the Lord require of thee, but to do justly, and to love mercy, and to walk humbly with thy God?

### Micah 6:9 `mic6v9`

The Lord’s voice crieth unto the city, and the man of wisdom shall see thy name: hear ye the rod, and who hath appointed it.

### Micah 6:10 `mic6v10`

Are there yet the treasures of wickedness in the house of the wicked, and the scant measure that is abominable?

### Micah 6:11 `mic6v11`

Shall I count them pure with the wicked balances, and with the bag of deceitful weights?

### Micah 6:12 `mic6v12`

For the rich men thereof are full of violence, and the inhabitants thereof have spoken lies, and their tongue is deceitful in their mouth.

### Micah 6:13 `mic6v13`

Therefore also will I make thee sick in smiting thee, in making thee desolate because of thy sins.

### Micah 6:14 `mic6v14`

Thou shalt eat, but not be satisfied; and thy casting down shall be in the midst of thee; and thou shalt take hold, but shalt not deliver; and that which thou deliverest will I give up to the sword.

### Micah 6:15 `mic6v15`

Thou shalt sow, but thou shalt not reap; thou shalt tread the olives, but thou shalt not anoint thee with oil; and sweet wine, but shalt not drink wine.

### Micah 6:16 `mic6v16`

For the statutes of Omri are kept, and all the works of the house of Ahab, and ye walk in their counsels; that I should make thee a desolation, and the inhabitants thereof an hissing: therefore ye shall bear the reproach of my people.
