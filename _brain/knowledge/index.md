# Knowledge

Semantic memory: scripture, the people who interpreted it, and what they
concluded. Every claim carries its citation to the text.

## Folders

* `scripture/` — the Bible at chapter granularity, one document per chapter,
  verses addressable by atomic reference token (see
  `../conventions/addressing-scripture.md`). Translations are clustered on the
  chapter rather than split into parallel trees.
* `theologians/` — the significant figures of church history across
  traditions, their central positions linked to the chapters they rest on.
* `traditions/` — Orthodox, Catholic, Lutheran, Reformed, Pentecostal,
  Adventist, Episcopal and the rest: what each holds, and where it parts from
  the others.
* `doctrines/` — the contested topics themselves, each gathering the positions
  taken on it and the texts they argue from.
* `councils/` — ecumenical and confessional statements, as primary sources.
