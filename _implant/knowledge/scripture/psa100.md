Part of [Psalms 100](psa/psa.md).

Verses are addressed by atomic reference token — `psa100v1` is Psalms 100:1 — because the search tokenizer splits `Psalms 100:1` into separate terms and cannot then tell it from `Psalms 1:100`. See [addressing scripture](../../conventions/addressing-scripture.md).

## KJV — Psalms 100

### Psalms 100:1 `psa100v1`

A Psalm of praise. Make a joyful noise unto the Lord, all ye lands.

### Psalms 100:2 `psa100v2`

Serve the Lord with gladness: come before his presence with singing.

### Psalms 100:3 `psa100v3`

Know ye that the Lord he is God: it is he that hath made us, and not we ourselves; we are his people, and the sheep of his pasture.

### Psalms 100:4 `psa100v4`

Enter into his gates with thanksgiving, and into his courts with praise: be thankful unto him, and bless his name.

### Psalms 100:5 `psa100v5`

For the Lord is good; his mercy is everlasting; and his truth endureth to all generations.


## PR1938 — Psalms 100

### Psalms 100:2 `psa100v2`

### Psalms 100:3 `psa100v3`

Tietäkää, että Herra on Jumala. Hän on meidät tehnyt, ja hänen me olemme, hänen kansansa ja hänen laitumensa lampaat.

### Psalms 100:4 `psa100v4`

Käykää hänen portteihinsa kiittäen, hänen esikartanoihinsa ylistystä veisaten. Ylistäkää häntä, kiittäkää hänen nimeänsä.

Sillä Herra on hyvä; hänen armonsa pysyy iankaikkisesti ja hänen uskollisuutensa polvesta polveen.
