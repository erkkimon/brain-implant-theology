## KJV — Psalms 66

### Psalms 66:1 `psa66v1`

To the chief Musician, A Song or Psalm. Make a joyful noise unto God, all ye lands:

### Psalms 66:2 `psa66v2`

Sing forth the honour of his name: make his praise glorious.

### Psalms 66:3 `psa66v3`

Say unto God, How terrible art thou in thy works! through the greatness of thy power shall thine enemies submit themselves unto thee.

### Psalms 66:4 `psa66v4`

All the earth shall worship thee, and shall sing unto thee; they shall sing to thy name. Selah.

### Psalms 66:5 `psa66v5`

Come and see the works of God: he is terrible in his doing toward the children of men.

### Psalms 66:6 `psa66v6`

He turned the sea into dry land: they went through the flood on foot: there did we rejoice in him.

### Psalms 66:7 `psa66v7`

He ruleth by his power for ever; his eyes behold the nations: let not the rebellious exalt themselves. Selah.

### Psalms 66:8 `psa66v8`

O bless our God, ye people, and make the voice of his praise to be heard:

### Psalms 66:9 `psa66v9`

Which holdeth our soul in life, and suffereth not our feet to be moved.

### Psalms 66:10 `psa66v10`

For thou, O God, hast proved us: thou hast tried us, as silver is tried.

### Psalms 66:11 `psa66v11`

Thou broughtest us into the net; thou laidst affliction upon our loins.

### Psalms 66:12 `psa66v12`

Thou hast caused men to ride over our heads; we went through fire and through water: but thou broughtest us out into a wealthy place.

### Psalms 66:13 `psa66v13`

I will go into thy house with burnt offerings: I will pay thee my vows,

### Psalms 66:14 `psa66v14`

Which my lips have uttered, and my mouth hath spoken, when I was in trouble.

### Psalms 66:15 `psa66v15`

I will offer unto thee burnt sacrifices of fatlings, with the incense of rams; I will offer bullocks with goats. Selah.

### Psalms 66:16 `psa66v16`

Come and hear, all ye that fear God, and I will declare what he hath done for my soul.

### Psalms 66:17 `psa66v17`

I cried unto him with my mouth, and he was extolled with my tongue.

### Psalms 66:18 `psa66v18`

If I regard iniquity in my heart, the Lord will not hear me:

### Psalms 66:19 `psa66v19`

But verily God hath heard me; he hath attended to the voice of my prayer.

### Psalms 66:20 `psa66v20`

Blessed be God, which hath not turned away my prayer, nor his mercy from me.
