## KJV — Psalms 38

### Psalms 38:1 `psa38v1`

A Psalm of David, to bring to remembrance. O Lord, rebuke me not in thy wrath: neither chasten me in thy hot displeasure.

### Psalms 38:2 `psa38v2`

For thine arrows stick fast in me, and thy hand presseth me sore.

### Psalms 38:3 `psa38v3`

There is no soundness in my flesh because of thine anger; neither is there any rest in my bones because of my sin.

### Psalms 38:4 `psa38v4`

For mine iniquities are gone over mine head: as an heavy burden they are too heavy for me.

### Psalms 38:5 `psa38v5`

My wounds stink and are corrupt because of my foolishness.

### Psalms 38:6 `psa38v6`

I am troubled; I am bowed down greatly; I go mourning all the day long.

### Psalms 38:7 `psa38v7`

For my loins are filled with a loathsome disease: and there is no soundness in my flesh.

### Psalms 38:8 `psa38v8`

I am feeble and sore broken: I have roared by reason of the disquietness of my heart.

### Psalms 38:9 `psa38v9`

Lord, all my desire is before thee; and my groaning is not hid from thee.

### Psalms 38:10 `psa38v10`

My heart panteth, my strength faileth me: as for the light of mine eyes, it also is gone from me.

### Psalms 38:11 `psa38v11`

My lovers and my friends stand aloof from my sore; and my kinsmen stand afar off.

### Psalms 38:12 `psa38v12`

They also that seek after my life lay snares for me: and they that seek my hurt speak mischievous things, and imagine deceits all the day long.

### Psalms 38:13 `psa38v13`

But I, as a deaf man, heard not; and I was as a dumb man that openeth not his mouth.

### Psalms 38:14 `psa38v14`

Thus I was as a man that heareth not, and in whose mouth are no reproofs.

### Psalms 38:15 `psa38v15`

For in thee, O Lord, do I hope: thou wilt hear, O Lord my God.

### Psalms 38:16 `psa38v16`

For I said, Hear me, lest otherwise they should rejoice over me: when my foot slippeth, they magnify themselves against me.

### Psalms 38:17 `psa38v17`

For I am ready to halt, and my sorrow is continually before me.

### Psalms 38:18 `psa38v18`

For I will declare mine iniquity; I will be sorry for my sin.

### Psalms 38:19 `psa38v19`

But mine enemies are lively, and they are strong: and they that hate me wrongfully are multiplied.

### Psalms 38:20 `psa38v20`

They also that render evil for good are mine adversaries; because I follow the thing that good is.

### Psalms 38:21 `psa38v21`

Forsake me not, O Lord: O my God, be not far from me.

### Psalms 38:22 `psa38v22`

Make haste to help me, O Lord my salvation.
