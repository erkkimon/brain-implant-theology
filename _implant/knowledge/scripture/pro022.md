## KJV — Proverbs 22

### Proverbs 22:1 `pro22v1`

A good name is rather to be chosen than great riches, and loving favour rather than silver and gold.

### Proverbs 22:2 `pro22v2`

The rich and poor meet together: the Lord is the maker of them all.

### Proverbs 22:3 `pro22v3`

A prudent man foreseeth the evil, and hideth himself: but the simple pass on, and are punished.

### Proverbs 22:4 `pro22v4`

By humility and the fear of the Lord are riches, and honour, and life.

### Proverbs 22:5 `pro22v5`

Thorns and snares are in the way of the froward: he that doth keep his soul shall be far from them.

### Proverbs 22:6 `pro22v6`

Train up a child in the way he should go: and when he is old, he will not depart from it.

### Proverbs 22:7 `pro22v7`

The rich ruleth over the poor, and the borrower is servant to the lender.

### Proverbs 22:8 `pro22v8`

He that soweth iniquity shall reap vanity: and the rod of his anger shall fail.

### Proverbs 22:9 `pro22v9`

He that hath a bountiful eye shall be blessed; for he giveth of his bread to the poor.

### Proverbs 22:10 `pro22v10`

Cast out the scorner, and contention shall go out; yea, strife and reproach shall cease.

### Proverbs 22:11 `pro22v11`

He that loveth pureness of heart, for the grace of his lips the king shall be his friend.

### Proverbs 22:12 `pro22v12`

The eyes of the Lord preserve knowledge, and he overthroweth the words of the transgressor.

### Proverbs 22:13 `pro22v13`

The slothful man saith, There is a lion without, I shall be slain in the streets.

### Proverbs 22:14 `pro22v14`

The mouth of strange women is a deep pit: he that is abhorred of the Lord shall fall therein.

### Proverbs 22:15 `pro22v15`

Foolishness is bound in the heart of a child; but the rod of correction shall drive it far from him.

### Proverbs 22:16 `pro22v16`

He that oppresseth the poor to increase his riches, and he that giveth to the rich, shall surely come to want.

### Proverbs 22:17 `pro22v17`

Bow down thine ear, and hear the words of the wise, and apply thine heart unto my knowledge.

### Proverbs 22:18 `pro22v18`

For it is a pleasant thing if thou keep them within thee; they shall withal be fitted in thy lips.

### Proverbs 22:19 `pro22v19`

That thy trust may be in the Lord, I have made known to thee this day, even to thee.

### Proverbs 22:20 `pro22v20`

Have not I written to thee excellent things in counsels and knowledge,

### Proverbs 22:21 `pro22v21`

That I might make thee know the certainty of the words of truth; that thou mightest answer the words of truth to them that send unto thee?

### Proverbs 22:22 `pro22v22`

Rob not the poor, because he is poor: neither oppress the afflicted in the gate:

### Proverbs 22:23 `pro22v23`

For the Lord will plead their cause, and spoil the soul of those that spoiled them.

### Proverbs 22:24 `pro22v24`

Make no friendship with an angry man; and with a furious man thou shalt not go:

### Proverbs 22:25 `pro22v25`

Lest thou learn his ways, and get a snare to thy soul.

### Proverbs 22:26 `pro22v26`

Be not thou one of them that strike hands, or of them that are sureties for debts.

### Proverbs 22:27 `pro22v27`

If thou hast nothing to pay, why should he take away thy bed from under thee?

### Proverbs 22:28 `pro22v28`

Remove not the ancient landmark, which thy fathers have set.

### Proverbs 22:29 `pro22v29`

Seest thou a man diligent in his business? he shall stand before kings; he shall not stand before mean men.
