## KJV — Ezekiel 28

### Ezekiel 28:1 `ezk28v1`

The word of the Lord came again unto me, saying,

### Ezekiel 28:2 `ezk28v2`

Son of man, say unto the prince of Tyrus, Thus saith the Lord God; Because thine heart is lifted up, and thou hast said, I am a God, I sit in the seat of God, in the midst of the seas; yet thou art a man, and not God, though thou set thine heart as the heart of God:

### Ezekiel 28:3 `ezk28v3`

Behold, thou art wiser than Daniel; there is no secret that they can hide from thee:

### Ezekiel 28:5 `ezk28v5`

### Ezekiel 28:6 `ezk28v6`

### Ezekiel 28:7 `ezk28v7`

Behold, therefore I will bring strangers upon thee, the terrible of the nations: and they shall draw their swords against the beauty of thy wisdom, and they shall defile thy brightness.

### Ezekiel 28:8 `ezk28v8`

They shall bring thee down to the pit, and thou shalt die the deaths of them that are slain in the midst of the seas.

### Ezekiel 28:9 `ezk28v9`

Wilt thou yet say before him that slayeth thee, I am God? but thou shalt be a man, and no God, in the hand of him that slayeth thee.

### Ezekiel 28:10 `ezk28v10`

Thou shalt die the deaths of the uncircumcised by the hand of strangers: for I have spoken it, saith the Lord God.

### Ezekiel 28:11 `ezk28v11`

Moreover the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,

### Ezekiel 28:12 `ezk28v12`

Son of man, take up a lamentation upon the king of Tyrus, and say unto him, Thus saith the Lord God; Thou sealest up the sum, full of wisdom, and perfect in beauty.

### Ezekiel 28:13 `ezk28v13`

Thou hast been in Eden the garden of God; every precious stone was thy covering, the sardius, topaz, and the diamond, the beryl, the onyx, and the jasper, the sapphire, the emerald, and the carbuncle, and gold: the workmanship of thy tabrets and of thy pipes was prepared in thee in the day that thou wast created.

### Ezekiel 28:14 `ezk28v14`

Thou art the anointed cherub that covereth; and I have set thee so: thou wast upon the holy mountain of God; thou hast walked up and down in the midst of the stones of fire.

### Ezekiel 28:15 `ezk28v15`

Thou wast perfect in thy ways from the day that thou wast created, till iniquity was found in thee.

### Ezekiel 28:16 `ezk28v16`

### Ezekiel 28:17 `ezk28v17`

Thine heart was lifted up because of thy beauty, thou hast corrupted thy wisdom by reason of thy brightness: I will cast thee to the ground, I will lay thee before kings, that they may behold thee.

### Ezekiel 28:18 `ezk28v18`

Thou hast defiled thy sanctuaries by the multitude of thine iniquities, by the iniquity of thy traffick; therefore will I bring forth a fire from the midst of thee, it shall devour thee, and I will bring thee to ashes upon the earth in the sight of all them that behold thee.

### Ezekiel 28:19 `ezk28v19`

All they that know thee among the people shall be astonished at thee: thou shalt be a terror, and never shalt thou be any more.

### Ezekiel 28:20 `ezk28v20`

### Ezekiel 28:21 `ezk28v21`

### Ezekiel 28:22 `ezk28v22`

### Ezekiel 28:23 `ezk28v23`

### Ezekiel 28:24 `ezk28v24`

And there shall be no more a pricking brier unto the house of Israel, nor any grieving thorn of all that are round about them, that despised them; and they shall know that I am the Lord God.
