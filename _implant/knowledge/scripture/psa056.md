## KJV — Psalms 56

### Psalms 56:1 `psa56v1`

To the chief Musician upon Jonath–elem–rechokim, Michtam of David, when the Philistines took him in Gath. Be merciful unto me, O God: for man would swallow me up; he fighting daily oppresseth me.

### Psalms 56:2 `psa56v2`

Mine enemies would daily swallow me up: for they be many that fight against me, O thou most High.

### Psalms 56:3 `psa56v3`

What time I am afraid, I will trust in thee.

### Psalms 56:4 `psa56v4`

In God I will praise his word, in God I have put my trust; I will not fear what flesh can do unto me.

### Psalms 56:5 `psa56v5`

Every day they wrest my words: all their thoughts are against me for evil.

### Psalms 56:6 `psa56v6`

They gather themselves together, they hide themselves, they mark my steps, when they wait for my soul.

### Psalms 56:7 `psa56v7`

Shall they escape by iniquity? in thine anger cast down the people, O God.

### Psalms 56:8 `psa56v8`

Thou tellest my wanderings: put thou my tears into thy bottle: are they not in thy book?

### Psalms 56:9 `psa56v9`

When I cry unto thee, then shall mine enemies turn back: this I know; for God is for me.

### Psalms 56:10 `psa56v10`

In God will I praise his word: in the Lord will I praise his word.

### Psalms 56:11 `psa56v11`

In God have I put my trust: I will not be afraid what man can do unto me.

### Psalms 56:12 `psa56v12`

Thy vows are upon me, O God: I will render praises unto thee.

### Psalms 56:13 `psa56v13`

For thou hast delivered my soul from death: wilt not thou deliver my feet from falling, that I may walk before God in the light of the living?


## PR1938 — Psalms 56

### Psalms 56:1 `psa56v1`

Veisuunjohtajalle; veisataan kuin: "Mykkä kyyhkynen kaukaisessa maassa". Daavidin laulu, kun filistealaiset ottivat hänet kiinni Gatissa. {56:2} Jumala, ole minulle armollinen, sillä ihmiset minua polkevat, joka päivä he sotivat minua vastaan, ahdistavat minua.

### Psalms 56:2 `psa56v2`

{56:3} Joka päivä minun vihamieheni minua polkevat; sillä paljon on niitä, jotka ylpeästi sotivat minua vastaan.

### Psalms 56:3 `psa56v3`

{56:4} Mutta sinä päivänä, jota minä pelkään, minä turvaan sinuun.

### Psalms 56:4 `psa56v4`

### Psalms 56:5 `psa56v5`

{56:6} Joka päivä he vääntelevät minun sanojani, kaikki heidän ajatuksensa tarkoittavat minulle pahaa.

### Psalms 56:6 `psa56v6`

### Psalms 56:8 `psa56v8`
