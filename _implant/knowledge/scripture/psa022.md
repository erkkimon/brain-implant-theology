## KJV — Psalms 22

### Psalms 22:1 `psa22v1`

To the chief Musician upon Aijeleth Shahar, A Psalm of David. My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me? why art thou so far from helping me, and from the words of my roaring?

### Psalms 22:2 `psa22v2`

O my God, I cry in the daytime, but thou hearest not; and in the night season, and am not silent.

### Psalms 22:3 `psa22v3`

But thou art holy, O thou that inhabitest the praises of Israel.

### Psalms 22:4 `psa22v4`

Our fathers trusted in thee: they trusted, and thou didst deliver them.

### Psalms 22:5 `psa22v5`

They cried unto thee, and were delivered: they trusted in thee, and were not confounded.

### Psalms 22:6 `psa22v6`

But I am a worm, and no man; a reproach of men, and despised of the people.

### Psalms 22:7 `psa22v7`

All they that see me laugh me to scorn: they shoot out the lip, they shake the head, saying,

### Psalms 22:8 `psa22v8`

He trusted on the Lord that he would deliver him: let him deliver him, seeing he delighted in him.

### Psalms 22:9 `psa22v9`

But thou art he that took me out of the womb: thou didst make me hope when I was upon my mother’s breasts.

### Psalms 22:10 `psa22v10`

I was cast upon thee from the womb: thou art my God from my mother’s belly.

### Psalms 22:11 `psa22v11`

Be not far from me; for trouble is near; for there is none to help.

### Psalms 22:12 `psa22v12`

Many bulls have compassed me: strong bulls of Bashan have beset me round.

### Psalms 22:13 `psa22v13`

They gaped upon me with their mouths, as a ravening and a roaring lion.

### Psalms 22:14 `psa22v14`

I am poured out like water, and all my bones are out of joint: my heart is like wax; it is melted in the midst of my bowels.

### Psalms 22:15 `psa22v15`

My strength is dried up like a potsherd; and my tongue cleaveth to my jaws; and thou hast brought me into the dust of death.

### Psalms 22:16 `psa22v16`

For dogs have compassed me: the assembly of the wicked have inclosed me: they pierced my hands and my feet.

### Psalms 22:17 `psa22v17`

I may tell all my bones: they look and stare upon me.

### Psalms 22:18 `psa22v18`

They part my garments among them, and cast lots upon my vesture.

### Psalms 22:19 `psa22v19`

But be not thou far from me, O Lord: O my strength, haste thee to help me.

### Psalms 22:20 `psa22v20`

Deliver my soul from the sword; my darling from the power of the dog.

### Psalms 22:21 `psa22v21`

Save me from the lion’s mouth: for thou hast heard me from the horns of the unicorns.

### Psalms 22:22 `psa22v22`

I will declare thy name unto my brethren: in the midst of the congregation will I praise thee.

### Psalms 22:23 `psa22v23`

Ye that fear the Lord, praise him; all ye the seed of Jacob, glorify him; and fear him, all ye the seed of Israel.

### Psalms 22:24 `psa22v24`

For he hath not despised nor abhorred the affliction of the afflicted; neither hath he hid his face from him; but when he cried unto him, he heard.

### Psalms 22:25 `psa22v25`

My praise shall be of thee in the great congregation: I will pay my vows before them that fear him.

### Psalms 22:26 `psa22v26`

The meek shall eat and be satisfied: they shall praise the Lord that seek him: your heart shall live for ever.

### Psalms 22:27 `psa22v27`

All the ends of the world shall remember and turn unto the Lord: and all the kindreds of the nations shall worship before thee.

### Psalms 22:28 `psa22v28`

For the kingdom is the Lord’s: and he is the governor among the nations.

### Psalms 22:29 `psa22v29`

All they that be fat upon earth shall eat and worship: all they that go down to the dust shall bow before him: and none can keep alive his own soul.

### Psalms 22:30 `psa22v30`

A seed shall serve him; it shall be accounted to the Lord for a generation.

### Psalms 22:31 `psa22v31`

They shall come, and shall declare his righteousness unto a people that shall be born, that he hath done this.
