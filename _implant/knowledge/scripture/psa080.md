## KJV — Psalms 80

### Psalms 80:1 `psa80v1`

To the chief Musician upon Shoshannim–Eduth, A Psalm of Asaph. Give ear, O Shepherd of Israel, thou that leadest Joseph like a flock; thou that dwellest between the cherubims, shine forth.

### Psalms 80:2 `psa80v2`

Before Ephraim and Benjamin and Manasseh stir up thy strength, and come and save us.

### Psalms 80:3 `psa80v3`

Turn us again, O God, and cause thy face to shine; and we shall be saved.

### Psalms 80:4 `psa80v4`

O Lord God of hosts, how long wilt thou be angry against the prayer of thy people?

### Psalms 80:5 `psa80v5`

Thou feedest them with the bread of tears; and givest them tears to drink in great measure.

### Psalms 80:6 `psa80v6`

Thou makest us a strife unto our neighbours: and our enemies laugh among themselves.

### Psalms 80:7 `psa80v7`

Turn us again, O God of hosts, and cause thy face to shine; and we shall be saved.

### Psalms 80:8 `psa80v8`

Thou hast brought a vine out of Egypt: thou hast cast out the heathen, and planted it.

### Psalms 80:9 `psa80v9`

Thou preparedst room before it, and didst cause it to take deep root, and it filled the land.

### Psalms 80:10 `psa80v10`

The hills were covered with the shadow of it, and the boughs thereof were like the goodly cedars.

### Psalms 80:11 `psa80v11`

She sent out her boughs unto the sea, and her branches unto the river.

### Psalms 80:12 `psa80v12`

Why hast thou then broken down her hedges, so that all they which pass by the way do pluck her?

### Psalms 80:13 `psa80v13`

The boar out of the wood doth waste it, and the wild beast of the field doth devour it.

### Psalms 80:14 `psa80v14`

Return, we beseech thee, O God of hosts: look down from heaven, and behold, and visit this vine;

### Psalms 80:15 `psa80v15`

And the vineyard which thy right hand hath planted, and the branch that thou madest strong for thyself.

### Psalms 80:16 `psa80v16`

It is burned with fire, it is cut down: they perish at the rebuke of thy countenance.

### Psalms 80:17 `psa80v17`

Let thy hand be upon the man of thy right hand, upon the son of man whom thou madest strong for thyself.

### Psalms 80:18 `psa80v18`

So will not we go back from thee: quicken us, and we will call upon thy name.

### Psalms 80:19 `psa80v19`

Turn us again, O Lord God of hosts, cause thy face to shine; and we shall be saved.
