## KJV — Deuteronomy 17

### Deuteronomy 17:1 `deu17v1`

Thou shalt not sacrifice unto the Lord thy God any bullock, or sheep, wherein is blemish, or any evilfavouredness: for that is an abomination unto the Lord thy God.

### Deuteronomy 17:2 `deu17v2`

If there be found among you, within any of thy gates which the Lord thy God giveth thee, man or woman, that hath wrought wickedness in the sight of the Lord thy God, in transgressing his covenant,

### Deuteronomy 17:3 `deu17v3`

And hath gone and served other gods, and worshipped them, either the sun, or moon, or any of the host of heaven, which I have not commanded;

### Deuteronomy 17:4 `deu17v4`

And it be told thee, and thou hast heard of it, and enquired diligently, and, behold, it be true, and the thing certain, that such abomination is wrought in Israel:

### Deuteronomy 17:5 `deu17v5`

Then shalt thou bring forth that man or that woman, which have committed that wicked thing, unto thy gates, even that man or that woman, and shalt stone them with stones, till they die.

### Deuteronomy 17:6 `deu17v6`

At the mouth of two witnesses, or three witnesses, shall he that is worthy of death be put to death; but at the mouth of one witness he shall not be put to death.

### Deuteronomy 17:7 `deu17v7`

The hands of the witnesses shall be first upon him to put him to death, and afterward the hands of all the people. So thou shalt put the evil away from among you.

### Deuteronomy 17:8 `deu17v8`

### Deuteronomy 17:9 `deu17v9`

And thou shalt come unto the priests the Levites, and unto the judge that shall be in those days, and enquire; and they shall shew thee the sentence of judgment:

### Deuteronomy 17:10 `deu17v10`

And thou shalt do according to the sentence, which they of that place which the Lord shall choose shall shew thee; and thou shalt observe to do according to all that they inform thee:

### Deuteronomy 17:11 `deu17v11`

According to the sentence of the law which they shall teach thee, and according to the judgment which they shall tell thee, thou shalt do: thou shalt not decline from the sentence which they shall shew thee, to the right hand, nor to the left.

### Deuteronomy 17:12 `deu17v12`

And the man that will do presumptuously, and will not hearken unto the priest that standeth to minister there before the Lord thy God, or unto the judge, even that man shall die: and thou shalt put away the evil from Israel.

### Deuteronomy 17:13 `deu17v13`

And all the people shall hear, and fear, and do no more presumptuously.

### Deuteronomy 17:14 `deu17v14`

### Deuteronomy 17:17 `deu17v17`

### Deuteronomy 17:18 `deu17v18`

### Deuteronomy 17:19 `deu17v19`

### Deuteronomy 17:20 `deu17v20`
