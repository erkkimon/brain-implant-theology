The word that came to Jeremiah from the Lord in the tenth year of Zedekiah king of Judah, which was the eighteenth year of Nebuchadrezzar.

For then the king of Babylon’s army besieged Jerusalem: and Jeremiah the prophet was shut up in the court of the prison, which was in the king of Judah’s house.

### Jeremiah 32:3 `jer32v3`

For Zedekiah king of Judah had shut him up, saying, Wherefore dost thou prophesy, and say, Thus saith the Lord, Behold, I will give this city into the hand of the king of Babylon, and he shall take it;

### Jeremiah 32:4 `jer32v4`

And Zedekiah king of Judah shall not escape out of the hand of the Chaldeans, but shall surely be delivered into the hand of the king of Babylon, and shall speak with him mouth to mouth, and his eyes shall behold his eyes;

### Jeremiah 32:5 `jer32v5`

And he shall lead Zedekiah to Babylon, and there shall he be until I visit him, saith the Lord: though ye fight with the Chaldeans, ye shall not prosper.

### Jeremiah 32:6 `jer32v6`

And Jeremiah said, The word of the Lord came unto me, saying,

### Jeremiah 32:7 `jer32v7`

Behold, Hanameel the son of Shallum thine uncle shall come unto thee, saying, Buy thee my field that is in Anathoth: for the right of redemption is thine to buy it.

Then I knew that this was the word of the Lord.

And I bought the field of Hanameel my uncle’s son, that was in Anathoth, and weighed him the money, even seventeen shekels of silver.

### Jeremiah 32:12 `jer32v12`

### Jeremiah 32:22 `jer32v22`

### Jeremiah 32:36 `jer32v36`

### Jeremiah 32:37 `jer32v37`

### Jeremiah 32:39 `jer32v39`

And I will give them one heart, and one way, that they may fear me for ever, for the good of them, and of their children after them:

### Jeremiah 32:40 `jer32v40`

And I will make an everlasting covenant with them, that I will not turn away from them, to do them good; but I will put my fear in their hearts, that they shall not depart from me.

### Jeremiah 32:41 `jer32v41`

Yea, I will rejoice over them to do them good, and I will plant them in this land assuredly with my whole heart and with my whole soul.

### Jeremiah 32:42 `jer32v42`

### Jeremiah 32:5 `jer32v5`
