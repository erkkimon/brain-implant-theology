## KJV — Hosea 12

### Hosea 12:1 `hos12v1`

Ephraim feedeth on wind, and followeth after the east wind: he daily increaseth lies and desolation; and they do make a covenant with the Assyrians, and oil is carried into Egypt.

### Hosea 12:2 `hos12v2`

The Lord hath also a controversy with Judah, and will punish Jacob according to his ways; according to his doings will he recompense him.

### Hosea 12:3 `hos12v3`

He took his brother by the heel in the womb, and by his strength he had power with God:

### Hosea 12:4 `hos12v4`

Yea, he had power over the angel, and prevailed: he wept, and made supplication unto him: he found him in Beth–el, and there he spake with us;

### Hosea 12:5 `hos12v5`

Even the Lord God of hosts; the Lord is his memorial.

### Hosea 12:6 `hos12v6`

Therefore turn thou to thy God: keep mercy and judgment, and wait on thy God continually.

### Hosea 12:7 `hos12v7`

He is a merchant, the balances of deceit are in his hand: he loveth to oppress.

### Hosea 12:8 `hos12v8`

And Ephraim said, Yet I am become rich, I have found me out substance: in all my labours they shall find none iniquity in me that were sin.

### Hosea 12:9 `hos12v9`

And I that am the Lord thy God from the land of Egypt will yet make thee to dwell in tabernacles, as in the days of the solemn feast.

### Hosea 12:10 `hos12v10`

I have also spoken by the prophets, and I have multiplied visions, and used similitudes, by the ministry of the prophets.

### Hosea 12:11 `hos12v11`

Is there iniquity in Gilead? surely they are vanity: they sacrifice bullocks in Gilgal; yea, their altars are as heaps in the furrows of the fields.

### Hosea 12:12 `hos12v12`

And Jacob fled into the country of Syria, and Israel served for a wife, and for a wife he kept sheep.

### Hosea 12:13 `hos12v13`

And by a prophet the Lord brought Israel out of Egypt, and by a prophet was he preserved.

### Hosea 12:14 `hos12v14`

Ephraim provoked him to anger most bitterly: therefore shall he leave his blood upon him, and his reproach shall his Lord return unto him.


## PR1938 — Hosea 12

### Hosea 12:2 `hos12v2`

{12:3} Mutta Herralla on oikeudenkäynti Juudan kanssa, ja hän tahtoo rangaista Jaakobia sen teitten mukaan, hän on kostava sille sen tekojen mukaan.

### Hosea 12:3 `hos12v3`

{12:4} Äidin kohdussa tämä petti veljensä, ja miehuutensa voimassa hän taisteli Jumalan kanssa.

### Hosea 12:4 `hos12v4`

{12:5} Hän taisteli enkelin kanssa ja voitti, hän itki ja rukoili tätä. Beetelissä tämä löysi hänet ja puhui siellä meidän kanssamme.

### Hosea 12:5 `hos12v5`

{12:6} Herra, Jumala Sebaot-Herra on hänen nimensä.

### Hosea 12:6 `hos12v6`

{12:7} Ja sinä-käänny Jumalasi tykö, noudata laupeutta ja oikeutta ja pane alati toivosi Jumalaasi.

### Hosea 12:7 `hos12v7`

{12:8} Kanaan-hänen kädessänsä on petollinen vaaka, halusta hän tekee vääryyttä.

{12:9} Efraim sanoo: "Olenpas rikastunut, olen saanut itselleni omaisuutta; ei mikään minun työansioni tuota minulle syyllisyyttä, joka olisi syntiä".

### Hosea 12:13 `hos12v13`
