## KJV — Psalms 115

### Psalms 115:1 `psa115v1`

Not unto us, O Lord, not unto us, but unto thy name give glory, for thy mercy, and for thy truth’s sake.

### Psalms 115:2 `psa115v2`

Wherefore should the heathen say, Where is now their God?

### Psalms 115:3 `psa115v3`

But our God is in the heavens: he hath done whatsoever he hath pleased.

### Psalms 115:4 `psa115v4`

Their idols are silver and gold, the work of men’s hands.

### Psalms 115:5 `psa115v5`

They have mouths, but they speak not: eyes have they, but they see not:

### Psalms 115:6 `psa115v6`

They have ears, but they hear not: noses have they, but they smell not:

They have hands, but they handle not: feet have they, but they walk not: neither speak they through their throat.

### Psalms 115:8 `psa115v8`

They that make them are like unto them; so is every one that trusteth in them.

### Psalms 115:9 `psa115v9`

O Israel, trust thou in the Lord: he is their help and their shield.

### Psalms 115:10 `psa115v10`

O house of Aaron, trust in the Lord: he is their help and their shield.

### Psalms 115:11 `psa115v11`

Ye that fear the Lord, trust in the Lord: he is their help and their shield.

### Psalms 115:12 `psa115v12`

The Lord hath been mindful of us: he will bless us; he will bless the house of Israel; he will bless the house of Aaron.

### Psalms 115:13 `psa115v13`

He will bless them that fear the Lord, both small and great.

### Psalms 115:14 `psa115v14`

The Lord shall increase you more and more, you and your children.

### Psalms 115:15 `psa115v15`

Ye are blessed of the Lord which made heaven and earth.

### Psalms 115:16 `psa115v16`

The heaven, even the heavens, are the Lord’s: but the earth hath he given to the children of men.

### Psalms 115:17 `psa115v17`

The dead praise not the Lord, neither any that go down into silence.

### Psalms 115:18 `psa115v18`

But we will bless the Lord from this time forth and for evermore. Praise the Lord.
