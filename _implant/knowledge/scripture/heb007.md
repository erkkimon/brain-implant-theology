## KJV — Hebrews 7

### Hebrews 7:1 `heb7v1`

For this Melchisedec, king of Salem, priest of the most high God, who met Abraham returning from the slaughter of the kings, and blessed him;

### Hebrews 7:2 `heb7v2`

To whom also Abraham gave a tenth part of all; first being by interpretation King of righteousness, and after that also King of Salem, which is, King of peace;

### Hebrews 7:3 `heb7v3`

Without father, without mother, without descent, having neither beginning of days, nor end of life; but made like unto the Son of God; abideth a priest continually.

### Hebrews 7:4 `heb7v4`

Now consider how great this man was, unto whom even the patriarch Abraham gave the tenth of the spoils.

### Hebrews 7:5 `heb7v5`

### Hebrews 7:6 `heb7v6`

But he whose descent is not counted from them received tithes of Abraham, and blessed him that had the promises.

### Hebrews 7:7 `heb7v7`

And without all contradiction the less is blessed of the better.

### Hebrews 7:8 `heb7v8`

And here men that die receive tithes; but there he receiveth them, of whom it is witnessed that he liveth.

### Hebrews 7:9 `heb7v9`

And as I may so say, Levi also, who receiveth tithes, payed tithes in Abraham.

### Hebrews 7:10 `heb7v10`

### Hebrews 7:11 `heb7v11`

### Hebrews 7:12 `heb7v12`

For the priesthood being changed, there is made of necessity a change also of the law.

### Hebrews 7:13 `heb7v13`

For he of whom these things are spoken pertaineth to another tribe, of which no man gave attendance at the altar.

### Hebrews 7:14 `heb7v14`

For it is evident that our Lord sprang out of Juda; of which tribe Moses spake nothing concerning priesthood.

### Hebrews 7:15 `heb7v15`

### Hebrews 7:16 `heb7v16`

Who is made, not after the law of a carnal commandment, but after the power of an endless life.

### Hebrews 7:17 `heb7v17`

For he testifieth, Thou art a priest for ever after the order of Melchisedec.

### Hebrews 7:18 `heb7v18`

For there is verily a disannulling of the commandment going before for the weakness and unprofitableness thereof.

### Hebrews 7:19 `heb7v19`

For the law made nothing perfect, but the bringing in of a better hope did; by the which we draw nigh unto God.

### Hebrews 7:20 `heb7v20`

And inasmuch as not without an oath he was made priest:

### Hebrews 7:21 `heb7v21`

### Hebrews 7:22 `heb7v22`

By so much was Jesus made a surety of a better testament.

### Hebrews 7:23 `heb7v23`

And they truly were many priests, because they were not suffered to continue by reason of death:

### Hebrews 7:24 `heb7v24`

But this man, because he continueth ever, hath an unchangeable priesthood.

### Hebrews 7:25 `heb7v25`

### Hebrews 7:26 `heb7v26`

### Hebrews 7:27 `heb7v27`
