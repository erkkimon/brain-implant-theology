## KJV — Psalms 49

### Psalms 49:1 `psa49v1`

To the chief Musician, A Psalm for the sons of Korah. Hear this, all ye people; give ear, all ye inhabitants of the world:

### Psalms 49:2 `psa49v2`

Both low and high, rich and poor, together.

### Psalms 49:3 `psa49v3`

My mouth shall speak of wisdom; and the meditation of my heart shall be of understanding.

### Psalms 49:4 `psa49v4`

I will incline mine ear to a parable: I will open my dark saying upon the harp.

### Psalms 49:5 `psa49v5`

Wherefore should I fear in the days of evil, when the iniquity of my heels shall compass me about?

### Psalms 49:6 `psa49v6`

They that trust in their wealth, and boast themselves in the multitude of their riches;

### Psalms 49:7 `psa49v7`

None of them can by any means redeem his brother, nor give to God a ransom for him:

### Psalms 49:8 `psa49v8`

(For the redemption of their soul is precious, and it ceaseth for ever:)

### Psalms 49:9 `psa49v9`

That he should still live for ever, and not see corruption.

### Psalms 49:10 `psa49v10`

For he seeth that wise men die, likewise the fool and the brutish person perish, and leave their wealth to others.

### Psalms 49:11 `psa49v11`

Their inward thought is, that their houses shall continue for ever, and their dwelling places to all generations; they call their lands after their own names.

### Psalms 49:12 `psa49v12`

Nevertheless man being in honour abideth not: he is like the beasts that perish.

### Psalms 49:13 `psa49v13`

This their way is their folly: yet their posterity approve their sayings. Selah.

### Psalms 49:14 `psa49v14`

Like sheep they are laid in the grave; death shall feed on them; and the upright shall have dominion over them in the morning; and their beauty shall consume in the grave from their dwelling.

### Psalms 49:15 `psa49v15`

But God will redeem my soul from the power of the grave: for he shall receive me. Selah.

### Psalms 49:16 `psa49v16`

Be not thou afraid when one is made rich, when the glory of his house is increased;

### Psalms 49:17 `psa49v17`

For when he dieth he shall carry nothing away: his glory shall not descend after him.

### Psalms 49:18 `psa49v18`

Though while he lived he blessed his soul: and men will praise thee, when thou doest well to thyself.

### Psalms 49:19 `psa49v19`

He shall go to the generation of his fathers; they shall never see light.

### Psalms 49:20 `psa49v20`

Man that is in honour, and understandeth not, is like the beasts that perish.
