## KJV — Psalms 74

### Psalms 74:1 `psa74v1`

Maschil of Asaph. O God, why hast thou cast us off for ever? why doth thine anger smoke against the sheep of thy pasture?

### Psalms 74:2 `psa74v2`

Remember thy congregation, which thou hast purchased of old; the rod of thine inheritance, which thou hast redeemed; this mount Zion, wherein thou hast dwelt.

### Psalms 74:3 `psa74v3`

Lift up thy feet unto the perpetual desolations; even all that the enemy hath done wickedly in the sanctuary.

### Psalms 74:4 `psa74v4`

Thine enemies roar in the midst of thy congregations; they set up their ensigns for signs.

### Psalms 74:5 `psa74v5`

A man was famous according as he had lifted up axes upon the thick trees.

### Psalms 74:6 `psa74v6`

But now they break down the carved work thereof at once with axes and hammers.

### Psalms 74:7 `psa74v7`

They have cast fire into thy sanctuary, they have defiled by casting down the dwelling place of thy name to the ground.

### Psalms 74:8 `psa74v8`

They said in their hearts, Let us destroy them together: they have burned up all the synagogues of God in the land.

### Psalms 74:9 `psa74v9`

We see not our signs: there is no more any prophet: neither is there among us any that knoweth how long.

### Psalms 74:10 `psa74v10`

O God, how long shall the adversary reproach? shall the enemy blaspheme thy name for ever?

### Psalms 74:11 `psa74v11`

Why withdrawest thou thy hand, even thy right hand? pluck it out of thy bosom.

### Psalms 74:12 `psa74v12`

For God is my King of old, working salvation in the midst of the earth.

### Psalms 74:13 `psa74v13`

Thou didst divide the sea by thy strength: thou brakest the heads of the dragons in the waters.

### Psalms 74:14 `psa74v14`

Thou brakest the heads of leviathan in pieces, and gavest him to be meat to the people inhabiting the wilderness.

### Psalms 74:15 `psa74v15`

Thou didst cleave the fountain and the flood: thou driedst up mighty rivers.

### Psalms 74:16 `psa74v16`

The day is thine, the night also is thine: thou hast prepared the light and the sun.

### Psalms 74:17 `psa74v17`

Thou hast set all the borders of the earth: thou hast made summer and winter.

### Psalms 74:18 `psa74v18`

Remember this, that the enemy hath reproached, O Lord, and that the foolish people have blasphemed thy name.

### Psalms 74:19 `psa74v19`

O deliver not the soul of thy turtledove unto the multitude of the wicked: forget not the congregation of thy poor for ever.

### Psalms 74:20 `psa74v20`

Have respect unto the covenant: for the dark places of the earth are full of the habitations of cruelty.

### Psalms 74:21 `psa74v21`

O let not the oppressed return ashamed: let the poor and needy praise thy name.

### Psalms 74:22 `psa74v22`

Arise, O God, plead thine own cause: remember how the foolish man reproacheth thee daily.

### Psalms 74:23 `psa74v23`

Forget not the voice of thine enemies: the tumult of those that rise up against thee increaseth continually.
