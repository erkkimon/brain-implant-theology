## KJV — Psalms 72

### Psalms 72:1 `psa72v1`

A Psalm for Solomon. Give the king thy judgments, O God, and thy righteousness unto the king’s son.

### Psalms 72:2 `psa72v2`

He shall judge thy people with righteousness, and thy poor with judgment.

### Psalms 72:3 `psa72v3`

The mountains shall bring peace to the people, and the little hills, by righteousness.

### Psalms 72:4 `psa72v4`

He shall judge the poor of the people, he shall save the children of the needy, and shall break in pieces the oppressor.

### Psalms 72:5 `psa72v5`

They shall fear thee as long as the sun and moon endure, throughout all generations.

### Psalms 72:6 `psa72v6`

He shall come down like rain upon the mown grass: as showers that water the earth.

### Psalms 72:7 `psa72v7`

In his days shall the righteous flourish; and abundance of peace so long as the moon endureth.

### Psalms 72:8 `psa72v8`

He shall have dominion also from sea to sea, and from the river unto the ends of the earth.

### Psalms 72:9 `psa72v9`

They that dwell in the wilderness shall bow before him; and his enemies shall lick the dust.

### Psalms 72:10 `psa72v10`

The kings of Tarshish and of the isles shall bring presents: the kings of Sheba and Seba shall offer gifts.

### Psalms 72:11 `psa72v11`

Yea, all kings shall fall down before him: all nations shall serve him.

### Psalms 72:12 `psa72v12`

For he shall deliver the needy when he crieth; the poor also, and him that hath no helper.

### Psalms 72:13 `psa72v13`

He shall spare the poor and needy, and shall save the souls of the needy.

### Psalms 72:14 `psa72v14`

He shall redeem their soul from deceit and violence: and precious shall their blood be in his sight.

### Psalms 72:15 `psa72v15`

And he shall live, and to him shall be given of the gold of Sheba: prayer also shall be made for him continually; and daily shall he be praised.

### Psalms 72:16 `psa72v16`

There shall be an handful of corn in the earth upon the top of the mountains; the fruit thereof shall shake like Lebanon: and they of the city shall flourish like grass of the earth.

### Psalms 72:17 `psa72v17`

His name shall endure for ever: his name shall be continued as long as the sun: and men shall be blessed in him: all nations shall call him blessed.

### Psalms 72:18 `psa72v18`

Blessed be the Lord God, the God of Israel, who only doeth wondrous things.

### Psalms 72:19 `psa72v19`

And blessed be his glorious name for ever: and let the whole earth be filled with his glory; Amen, and Amen.

### Psalms 72:20 `psa72v20`

The prayers of David the son of Jesse are ended.
