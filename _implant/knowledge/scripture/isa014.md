## KJV — Isaiah 14

### Isaiah 14:1 `isa14v1`

For the Lord will have mercy on Jacob, and will yet choose Israel, and set them in their own land: and the strangers shall be joined with them, and they shall cleave to the house of Jacob.

### Isaiah 14:2 `isa14v2`

And the people shall take them, and bring them to their place: and the house of Israel shall possess them in the land of the Lord for servants and handmaids: and they shall take them captives, whose captives they were; and they shall rule over their oppressors.

And it shall come to pass in the day that the Lord shall give thee rest from thy sorrow, and from thy fear, and from the hard bondage wherein thou wast made to serve,

That thou shalt take up this proverb against the king of Babylon, and say, How hath the oppressor ceased! the golden city ceased!

### Isaiah 14:5 `isa14v5`

The Lord hath broken the staff of the wicked, and the sceptre of the rulers.

### Isaiah 14:6 `isa14v6`

He who smote the people in wrath with a continual stroke, he that ruled the nations in anger, is persecuted, and none hindereth.

### Isaiah 14:7 `isa14v7`

The whole earth is at rest, and is quiet: they break forth into singing.

### Isaiah 14:8 `isa14v8`

Yea, the fir trees rejoice at thee, and the cedars of Lebanon, saying, Since thou art laid down, no feller is come up against us.

### Isaiah 14:9 `isa14v9`

Hell from beneath is moved for thee to meet thee at thy coming: it stirreth up the dead for thee, even all the chief ones of the earth; it hath raised up from their thrones all the kings of the nations.

### Isaiah 14:10 `isa14v10`

All they shall speak and say unto thee, Art thou also become weak as we? art thou become like unto us?

### Isaiah 14:11 `isa14v11`

Thy pomp is brought down to the grave, and the noise of thy viols: the worm is spread under thee, and the worms cover thee.

### Isaiah 14:12 `isa14v12`

How art thou fallen from heaven, O Lucifer, son of the morning! how art thou cut down to the ground, which didst weaken the nations!

### Isaiah 14:13 `isa14v13`

### Isaiah 14:14 `isa14v14`

I will ascend above the heights of the clouds; I will be like the most High.

### Isaiah 14:15 `isa14v15`

Yet thou shalt be brought down to hell, to the sides of the pit.

### Isaiah 14:16 `isa14v16`

They that see thee shall narrowly look upon thee, and consider thee, saying, Is this the man that made the earth to tremble, that did shake kingdoms;

### Isaiah 14:17 `isa14v17`

### Isaiah 14:18 `isa14v18`

### Isaiah 14:19 `isa14v19`

But thou art cast out of thy grave like an abominable branch, and as the raiment of those that are slain, thrust through with a sword, that go down to the stones of the pit; as a carcase trodden under feet.

### Isaiah 14:20 `isa14v20`

Thou shalt not be joined with them in burial, because thou hast destroyed thy land, and slain thy people: the seed of evildoers shall never be renowned.

### Isaiah 14:21 `isa14v21`

### Isaiah 14:22 `isa14v22`

### Isaiah 14:23 `isa14v23`

The Lord of hosts hath sworn, saying, Surely as I have thought, so shall it come to pass; and as I have purposed, so shall it stand:

### Isaiah 14:25 `isa14v25`

### Isaiah 14:26 `isa14v26`

This is the purpose that is purposed upon the whole earth: and this is the hand that is stretched out upon all the nations.

### Isaiah 14:27 `isa14v27`

### Isaiah 14:28 `isa14v28`

### Isaiah 14:29 `isa14v29`

Rejoice not thou, whole Palestina, because the rod of him that smote thee is broken: for out of the serpent’s root shall come forth a cockatrice, and his fruit shall be a fiery flying serpent.

### Isaiah 14:30 `isa14v30`

And the firstborn of the poor shall feed, and the needy shall lie down in safety: and I will kill thy root with famine, and he shall slay thy remnant.

### Isaiah 14:31 `isa14v31`

### Isaiah 14:32 `isa14v32`
