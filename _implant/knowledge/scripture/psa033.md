Part of [Psalms 33](psa/psa.md).

Verses are addressed by atomic reference token — `psa33v1` is Psalms 33:1 — because the search tokenizer splits `Psalms 33:1` into separate terms and cannot then tell it from `Psalms 1:33`. See [addressing scripture](../../conventions/addressing-scripture.md).

## KJV — Psalms 33

### Psalms 33:1 `psa33v1`

Rejoice in the Lord, O ye righteous: for praise is comely for the upright.

### Psalms 33:2 `psa33v2`

Praise the Lord with harp: sing unto him with the psaltery and an instrument of ten strings.

### Psalms 33:3 `psa33v3`

Sing unto him a new song; play skilfully with a loud noise.

### Psalms 33:4 `psa33v4`

For the word of the Lord is right; and all his works are done in truth.

### Psalms 33:5 `psa33v5`

He loveth righteousness and judgment: the earth is full of the goodness of the Lord.

### Psalms 33:6 `psa33v6`

By the word of the Lord were the heavens made; and all the host of them by the breath of his mouth.

### Psalms 33:7 `psa33v7`

He gathereth the waters of the sea together as an heap: he layeth up the depth in storehouses.

### Psalms 33:8 `psa33v8`

Let all the earth fear the Lord: let all the inhabitants of the world stand in awe of him.

### Psalms 33:9 `psa33v9`

For he spake, and it was done; he commanded, and it stood fast.

### Psalms 33:10 `psa33v10`

The Lord bringeth the counsel of the heathen to nought: he maketh the devices of the people of none effect.

### Psalms 33:11 `psa33v11`

The counsel of the Lord standeth for ever, the thoughts of his heart to all generations.

### Psalms 33:12 `psa33v12`

Blessed is the nation whose God is the Lord; and the people whom he hath chosen for his own inheritance.

### Psalms 33:13 `psa33v13`

The Lord looketh from heaven; he beholdeth all the sons of men.

### Psalms 33:14 `psa33v14`

From the place of his habitation he looketh upon all the inhabitants of the earth.

### Psalms 33:15 `psa33v15`

He fashioneth their hearts alike; he considereth all their works.

### Psalms 33:16 `psa33v16`

There is no king saved by the multitude of an host: a mighty man is not delivered by much strength.

### Psalms 33:17 `psa33v17`

An horse is a vain thing for safety: neither shall he deliver any by his great strength.

### Psalms 33:18 `psa33v18`

Behold, the eye of the Lord is upon them that fear him, upon them that hope in his mercy;

### Psalms 33:19 `psa33v19`

To deliver their soul from death, and to keep them alive in famine.

### Psalms 33:20 `psa33v20`

Our soul waiteth for the Lord: he is our help and our shield.

### Psalms 33:21 `psa33v21`

For our heart shall rejoice in him, because we have trusted in his holy name.

### Psalms 33:22 `psa33v22`

Let thy mercy, O Lord, be upon us, according as we hope in thee.
