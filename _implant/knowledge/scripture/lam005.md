## KJV — Lamentations 5

### Lamentations 5:1 `lam5v1`

Remember, O Lord, what is come upon us: consider, and behold our reproach.

### Lamentations 5:2 `lam5v2`

Our inheritance is turned to strangers, our houses to aliens.

### Lamentations 5:3 `lam5v3`

We are orphans and fatherless, our mothers are as widows.

### Lamentations 5:4 `lam5v4`

We have drunken our water for money; our wood is sold unto us.

### Lamentations 5:5 `lam5v5`

Our necks are under persecution: we labour, and have no rest.

### Lamentations 5:6 `lam5v6`

We have given the hand to the Egyptians, and to the Assyrians, to be satisfied with bread.

### Lamentations 5:7 `lam5v7`

Our fathers have sinned, and are not; and we have borne their iniquities.

### Lamentations 5:8 `lam5v8`

Servants have ruled over us: there is none that doth deliver us out of their hand.

### Lamentations 5:9 `lam5v9`

We gat our bread with the peril of our lives because of the sword of the wilderness.

### Lamentations 5:10 `lam5v10`

Our skin was black like an oven because of the terrible famine.

### Lamentations 5:11 `lam5v11`

They ravished the women in Zion, and the maids in the cities of Judah.

### Lamentations 5:12 `lam5v12`

Princes are hanged up by their hand: the faces of elders were not honoured.

### Lamentations 5:13 `lam5v13`

They took the young men to grind, and the children fell under the wood.

### Lamentations 5:14 `lam5v14`

The elders have ceased from the gate, the young men from their musick.

### Lamentations 5:15 `lam5v15`

The joy of our heart is ceased; our dance is turned into mourning.

### Lamentations 5:16 `lam5v16`

The crown is fallen from our head: woe unto us, that we have sinned!

### Lamentations 5:17 `lam5v17`

For this our heart is faint; for these things our eyes are dim.

### Lamentations 5:18 `lam5v18`

Because of the mountain of Zion, which is desolate, the foxes walk upon it.

### Lamentations 5:19 `lam5v19`

Thou, O Lord, remainest for ever; thy throne from generation to generation.

### Lamentations 5:20 `lam5v20`

Wherefore dost thou forget us for ever, and forsake us so long time?

### Lamentations 5:21 `lam5v21`

Turn thou us unto thee, O Lord, and we shall be turned; renew our days as of old.

### Lamentations 5:22 `lam5v22`

But thou hast utterly rejected us; thou art very wroth against us.
