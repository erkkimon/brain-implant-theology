Verses are addressed by atomic reference token — `psa6v1` is Psalms 6:1 — because the search tokenizer splits `Psalms 6:1` into separate terms and cannot then tell it from `Psalms 1:6`. See [addressing scripture](../../conventions/addressing-scripture.md).

## KJV — Psalms 6

### Psalms 6:1 `psa6v1`

To the chief Musician on Neginoth upon Sheminith, A Psalm of David. O Lord, rebuke me not in thine anger, neither chasten me in thy hot displeasure.

### Psalms 6:2 `psa6v2`

Have mercy upon me, O Lord; for I am weak: O Lord, heal me; for my bones are vexed.

### Psalms 6:3 `psa6v3`

My soul is also sore vexed: but thou, O Lord, how long?

### Psalms 6:4 `psa6v4`

Return, O Lord, deliver my soul: oh save me for thy mercies’ sake.

### Psalms 6:5 `psa6v5`

For in death there is no remembrance of thee: in the grave who shall give thee thanks?

### Psalms 6:6 `psa6v6`

I am weary with my groaning; all the night make I my bed to swim; I water my couch with my tears.

### Psalms 6:7 `psa6v7`

Mine eye is consumed because of grief; it waxeth old because of all mine enemies.

### Psalms 6:8 `psa6v8`

Depart from me, all ye workers of iniquity; for the Lord hath heard the voice of my weeping.

### Psalms 6:9 `psa6v9`

The Lord hath heard my supplication; the Lord will receive my prayer.

### Psalms 6:10 `psa6v10`

Let all mine enemies be ashamed and sore vexed: let them return and be ashamed suddenly.
