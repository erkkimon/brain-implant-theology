## KJV — Ezekiel 25

### Ezekiel 25:1 `ezk25v1`

The word of the Lord came again unto me, saying,

### Ezekiel 25:2 `ezk25v2`

Son of man, set thy face against the Ammonites, and prophesy against them;

### Ezekiel 25:3 `ezk25v3`

And say unto the Ammonites, Hear the word of the Lord God; Thus saith the Lord God; Because thou saidst, Aha, against my sanctuary, when it was profaned; and against the land of Israel, when it was desolate; and against the house of Judah, when they went into captivity;

### Ezekiel 25:4 `ezk25v4`

Behold, therefore I will deliver thee to the men of the east for a possession, and they shall set their palaces in thee, and make their dwellings in thee: they shall eat thy fruit, and they shall drink thy milk.

### Ezekiel 25:5 `ezk25v5`

And I will make Rabbah a stable for camels, and the Ammonites a couchingplace for flocks: and ye shall know that I am the Lord.

### Ezekiel 25:6 `ezk25v6`

For thus saith the Lord God; Because thou hast clapped thine hands, and stamped with the feet, and rejoiced in heart with all thy despite against the land of Israel;

### Ezekiel 25:7 `ezk25v7`

Behold, therefore I will stretch out mine hand upon thee, and will deliver thee for a spoil to the heathen; and I will cut thee off from the people, and I will cause thee to perish out of the countries: I will destroy thee; and thou shalt know that I am the Lord.

### Ezekiel 25:8 `ezk25v8`

Thus saith the Lord God; Because that Moab and Seir do say, Behold, the house of Judah is like unto all the heathen;

### Ezekiel 25:9 `ezk25v9`

Therefore, behold, I will open the side of Moab from the cities, from his cities which are on his frontiers, the glory of the country, Beth–jeshimoth, Baal–meon, and Kiriathaim,

### Ezekiel 25:10 `ezk25v10`

Unto the men of the east with the Ammonites, and will give them in possession, that the Ammonites may not be remembered among the nations.

### Ezekiel 25:11 `ezk25v11`

And I will execute judgments upon Moab; and they shall know that I am the Lord.

### Ezekiel 25:12 `ezk25v12`

Thus saith the Lord God; Because that Edom hath dealt against the house of Judah by taking vengeance, and hath greatly offended, and revenged himself upon them;

### Ezekiel 25:13 `ezk25v13`

Therefore thus saith the Lord God; I will also stretch out mine hand upon Edom, and will cut off man and beast from it; and I will make it desolate from Teman; and they of Dedan shall fall by the sword.

### Ezekiel 25:14 `ezk25v14`

And I will lay my vengeance upon Edom by the hand of my people Israel: and they shall do in Edom according to mine anger and according to my fury; and they shall know my vengeance, saith the Lord God.

### Ezekiel 25:15 `ezk25v15`

Thus saith the Lord God; Because the Philistines have dealt by revenge, and have taken vengeance with a despiteful heart, to destroy it for the old hatred;

### Ezekiel 25:16 `ezk25v16`

Therefore thus saith the Lord God; Behold, I will stretch out mine hand upon the Philistines, and I will cut off the Cherethims, and destroy the remnant of the sea coast.

### Ezekiel 25:17 `ezk25v17`

And I will execute great vengeance upon them with furious rebukes; and they shall know that I am the Lord, when I shall lay my vengeance upon them.
