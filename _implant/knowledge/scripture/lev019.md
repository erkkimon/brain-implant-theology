## KJV — Leviticus 19

### Leviticus 19:1 `lev19v1`

And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,

### Leviticus 19:2 `lev19v2`

Speak unto all the congregation of the children of Israel, and say unto them, Ye shall be holy: for I the Lord your God am holy.

### Leviticus 19:3 `lev19v3`

Ye shall fear every man his mother, and his father, and keep my sabbaths: I am the Lord your God.

### Leviticus 19:4 `lev19v4`

Turn ye not unto idols, nor make to yourselves molten gods: I am the Lord your God.

### Leviticus 19:5 `lev19v5`

And if ye offer a sacrifice of peace offerings unto the Lord, ye shall offer it at your own will.

### Leviticus 19:6 `lev19v6`

It shall be eaten the same day ye offer it, and on the morrow: and if ought remain until the third day, it shall be burnt in the fire.

### Leviticus 19:7 `lev19v7`

And if it be eaten at all on the third day, it is abominable; it shall not be accepted.

### Leviticus 19:8 `lev19v8`

Therefore every one that eateth it shall bear his iniquity, because he hath profaned the hallowed thing of the Lord: and that soul shall be cut off from among his people.

### Leviticus 19:9 `lev19v9`

And when ye reap the harvest of your land, thou shalt not wholly reap the corners of thy field, neither shalt thou gather the gleanings of thy harvest.

### Leviticus 19:10 `lev19v10`

And thou shalt not glean thy vineyard, neither shalt thou gather every grape of thy vineyard; thou shalt leave them for the poor and stranger: I am the Lord your God.

### Leviticus 19:11 `lev19v11`

Ye shall not steal, neither deal falsely, neither lie one to another.

### Leviticus 19:12 `lev19v12`

And ye shall not swear by my name falsely, neither shalt thou profane the name of thy God: I am the Lord.

### Leviticus 19:13 `lev19v13`

Thou shalt not defraud thy neighbour, neither rob him: the wages of him that is hired shall not abide with thee all night until the morning.

### Leviticus 19:14 `lev19v14`

Thou shalt not curse the deaf, nor put a stumblingblock before the blind, but shalt fear thy God: I am the Lord.

### Leviticus 19:15 `lev19v15`

Ye shall do no unrighteousness in judgment: thou shalt not respect the person of the poor, nor honour the person of the mighty: but in righteousness shalt thou judge thy neighbour.

### Leviticus 19:16 `lev19v16`

Thou shalt not go up and down as a talebearer among thy people: neither shalt thou stand against the blood of thy neighbour: I am the Lord.

### Leviticus 19:17 `lev19v17`

Thou shalt not hate thy brother in thine heart: thou shalt in any wise rebuke thy neighbour, and not suffer sin upon him.

### Leviticus 19:18 `lev19v18`

Thou shalt not avenge, nor bear any grudge against the children of thy people, but thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself: I am the Lord.

### Leviticus 19:19 `lev19v19`

Ye shall keep my statutes. Thou shalt not let thy cattle gender with a diverse kind: thou shalt not sow thy field with mingled seed: neither shall a garment mingled of linen and woollen come upon thee.

### Leviticus 19:20 `lev19v20`

And whosoever lieth carnally with a woman, that is a bondmaid, betrothed to an husband, and not at all redeemed, nor freedom given her; she shall be scourged; they shall not be put to death, because she was not free.

### Leviticus 19:21 `lev19v21`

And he shall bring his trespass offering unto the Lord, unto the door of the tabernacle of the congregation, even a ram for a trespass offering.

### Leviticus 19:22 `lev19v22`

And the priest shall make an atonement for him with the ram of the trespass offering before the Lord for his sin which he hath done: and the sin which he hath done shall be forgiven him.

### Leviticus 19:23 `lev19v23`

And when ye shall come into the land, and shall have planted all manner of trees for food, then ye shall count the fruit thereof as uncircumcised: three years shall it be as uncircumcised unto you: it shall not be eaten of.

### Leviticus 19:24 `lev19v24`

But in the fourth year all the fruit thereof shall be holy to praise the Lord withal.

### Leviticus 19:25 `lev19v25`

And in the fifth year shall ye eat of the fruit thereof, that it may yield unto you the increase thereof: I am the Lord your God.

### Leviticus 19:26 `lev19v26`

Ye shall not eat any thing with the blood: neither shall ye use enchantment, nor observe times.

### Leviticus 19:27 `lev19v27`

Ye shall not round the corners of your heads, neither shalt thou mar the corners of thy beard.

### Leviticus 19:28 `lev19v28`

Ye shall not make any cuttings in your flesh for the dead, nor print any marks upon you: I am the Lord.

### Leviticus 19:29 `lev19v29`

Do not prostitute thy daughter, to cause her to be a whore; lest the land fall to whoredom, and the land become full of wickedness.

### Leviticus 19:30 `lev19v30`

Ye shall keep my sabbaths, and reverence my sanctuary: I am the Lord.

### Leviticus 19:31 `lev19v31`

Regard not them that have familiar spirits, neither seek after wizards, to be defiled by them: I am the Lord your God.

### Leviticus 19:32 `lev19v32`

Thou shalt rise up before the hoary head, and honour the face of the old man, and fear thy God: I am the Lord.

### Leviticus 19:33 `lev19v33`

And if a stranger sojourn with thee in your land, ye shall not vex him.

### Leviticus 19:34 `lev19v34`

But the stranger that dwelleth with you shall be unto you as one born among you, and thou shalt love him as thyself; for ye were strangers in the land of Egypt: I am the Lord your God.

### Leviticus 19:35 `lev19v35`

Ye shall do no unrighteousness in judgment, in meteyard, in weight, or in measure.

### Leviticus 19:36 `lev19v36`

Just balances, just weights, a just ephah, and a just hin, shall ye have: I am the Lord your God, which brought you out of the land of Egypt.

### Leviticus 19:37 `lev19v37`

Therefore shall ye observe all my statutes, and all my judgments, and do them: I am the Lord.


## PR1938 — Leviticus 19

### Leviticus 19:1 `lev19v1`

Ja Herra puhui Moosekselle sanoen:

### Leviticus 19:2 `lev19v2`

"Puhu kaikelle israelilaisten seurakunnalle ja sano heille: Olkaa pyhät, sillä minä, Herra, teidän Jumalanne, olen pyhä.

### Leviticus 19:3 `lev19v3`

Jokainen teistä peljätköön äitiänsä ja isäänsä, ja pitäkää minun sapattini. Minä olen Herra, teidän Jumalanne.

### Leviticus 19:4 `lev19v4`

Älkää kääntykö epäjumalien puoleen älkääkä tehkö itsellenne valettuja jumalankuvia. Minä olen Herra, teidän Jumalanne.

### Leviticus 19:5 `lev19v5`

Ja kun te uhraatte yhteysuhria Herralle, uhratkaa se niin, että hänen mielisuosionsa tulee teidän osaksenne.

### Leviticus 19:6 `lev19v6`

Syökää se samana päivänä, jona te uhraatte, tai seuraavana päivänä; mutta mitä tähteeksi jää kolmanteen päivään, se poltettakoon tulessa.

### Leviticus 19:7 `lev19v7`

Jos sitä syödään kolmantena päivänä, niin se on saastaista, ei otollista.

### Leviticus 19:8 `lev19v8`

Ja joka sitä syö, joutuu syynalaiseksi, sillä hän on häväissyt Herran pyhän, ja hänet hävitettäköön kansastansa.

### Leviticus 19:9 `lev19v9`

Kun te korjaatte eloa maastanne, niin älä leikkaa viljaa pelloltasi reunoja myöten äläkä poimi tähkäpäitä leikkuun jälkeen.

### Leviticus 19:10 `lev19v10`

Älä myöskään korjaa tyhjäksi viinitarhaasi äläkä poimi varisseita marjoja, vaan jätä ne köyhälle ja muukalaiselle. Minä olen Herra, teidän Jumalanne.

### Leviticus 19:18 `lev19v18`

### Leviticus 19:19 `lev19v19`

### Leviticus 19:20 `lev19v20`

### Leviticus 19:21 `lev19v21`

### Leviticus 19:22 `lev19v22`

### Leviticus 19:23 `lev19v23`

Kun te tulette siihen maahan ja istutatte kaikkinaisia hedelmäpuita, niin jättäkää niiden hedelmä, niiden esinahka, koskemattomaksi; kolmena vuotena pitäkää ne ympärileikkaamattomina, älkää niitä syökö.

### Leviticus 19:24 `lev19v24`

### Leviticus 19:25 `lev19v25`
