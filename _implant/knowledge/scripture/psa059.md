## KJV — Psalms 59

### Psalms 59:1 `psa59v1`

To the chief Musician, Al–taschith, Michtam of David; when Saul sent, and they watched the house to kill him. Deliver me from mine enemies, O my God: defend me from them that rise up against me.

### Psalms 59:2 `psa59v2`

Deliver me from the workers of iniquity, and save me from bloody men.

### Psalms 59:3 `psa59v3`

For, lo, they lie in wait for my soul: the mighty are gathered against me; not for my transgression, nor for my sin, O Lord.

### Psalms 59:4 `psa59v4`

They run and prepare themselves without my fault: awake to help me, and behold.

### Psalms 59:5 `psa59v5`

Thou therefore, O Lord God of hosts, the God of Israel, awake to visit all the heathen: be not merciful to any wicked transgressors. Selah.

### Psalms 59:6 `psa59v6`

They return at evening: they make a noise like a dog, and go round about the city.

### Psalms 59:7 `psa59v7`

Behold, they belch out with their mouth: swords are in their lips: for who, say they, doth hear?

### Psalms 59:8 `psa59v8`

But thou, O Lord, shalt laugh at them; thou shalt have all the heathen in derision.

### Psalms 59:9 `psa59v9`

Because of his strength will I wait upon thee: for God is my defence.

### Psalms 59:10 `psa59v10`

The God of my mercy shall prevent me: God shall let me see my desire upon mine enemies.

### Psalms 59:11 `psa59v11`

Slay them not, lest my people forget: scatter them by thy power; and bring them down, O Lord our shield.

### Psalms 59:12 `psa59v12`

For the sin of their mouth and the words of their lips let them even be taken in their pride: and for cursing and lying which they speak.

### Psalms 59:13 `psa59v13`

Consume them in wrath, consume them, that they may not be: and let them know that God ruleth in Jacob unto the ends of the earth. Selah.

### Psalms 59:14 `psa59v14`

And at evening let them return; and let them make a noise like a dog, and go round about the city.

### Psalms 59:15 `psa59v15`

Let them wander up and down for meat, and grudge if they be not satisfied.

### Psalms 59:16 `psa59v16`

But I will sing of thy power; yea, I will sing aloud of thy mercy in the morning: for thou hast been my defence and refuge in the day of my trouble.

### Psalms 59:17 `psa59v17`

Unto thee, O my strength, will I sing: for God is my defence, and the God of my mercy.


## PR1938 — Psalms 59

Veisuunjohtajalle; veisataan kuin: "Älä turmele"; Daavidin laulu, kun Saul lähetti vartioimaan hänen taloansa, tappaakseen hänet. {59:2} Jumalani, pelasta minut vihollisistani, suojele minua niiltä, jotka nousevat minua vastaan.

### Psalms 59:2 `psa59v2`

{59:3} Pelasta minut pahantekijöistä, vapahda minut murhamiehistä.

### Psalms 59:4 `psa59v4`

{59:5} Ilman minun syytäni he juoksevat ja hankkiutuvat; heräjä, käy minua kohtaamaan ja katso.

### Psalms 59:9 `psa59v9`

{59:10} Sinä, minun väkevyyteni, sinusta minä otan vaarin, sillä Jumala on minun linnani.
