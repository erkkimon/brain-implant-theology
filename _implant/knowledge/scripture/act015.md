### Acts 15:2 `act15v2`

### Acts 15:4 `act15v4`

### Acts 15:5 `act15v5`

### Acts 15:6 `act15v6`

### Acts 15:8 `act15v8`

### Acts 15:9 `act15v9`

### Acts 15:10 `act15v10`

### Acts 15:11 `act15v11`

### Acts 15:12 `act15v12`

### Acts 15:13 `act15v13`

### Acts 15:14 `act15v14`

### Acts 15:16 `act15v16`

### Acts 15:17 `act15v17`

### Acts 15:22 `act15v22`

### Acts 15:23 `act15v23`

### Acts 15:24 `act15v24`

### Acts 15:25 `act15v25`
