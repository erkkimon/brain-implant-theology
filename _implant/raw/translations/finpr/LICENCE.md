# Raamattu 1933/38 (Kirkkoraamattu 1933/38) — licence

**Status: copyright expired. Free to redistribute.**

## The text

The Finnish church translation adopted by the Church Assembly: the Old
Testament in 1933, the New Testament in 1938. It was the official Finnish
Bible until Raamattu 1992.

## Why it is free

- The Finnish Bible Society, its publisher, states on its own translation
  page: *"Tekijänoikeus vanhentunut, lähioikeuksia digitaalisen edition
  korjauksiin ja nootituksiin Suomen Pipliaseura, 2017."* That is, the
  copyright has expired, and the Society holds neighbouring rights only to
  the corrections and notes of **its own 2017 digital edition**.
  https://www.raamattu.fi/kaannokset/raa (read 2026-09-27)
- DigiRaamattu records that the rights holder, Kirkon keskusrahasto, has
  released the rights and that the text may be used freely.
  http://ohje.digiraamattu.fi/index.php/Kirkkoraamattu_1933/38
- raamattu.uskonkirjat.net lists it as *vapaassa käytössä*, in free use
  (`../licence-audit.md`).

## This digital edition, and why the 2017 claim does not reach it

Obtained from `scrollmapper/bible_databases`
(https://github.com/scrollmapper/bible_databases), `formats/json/FinPR.json`,
labelled "FinPR: Finnish Pyhä Raamattu (1933/1938)". That repository
distributes its compilation under the **MIT Licence**. It is an older,
independent digitisation, not the Bible Society's 2017 edition, so none of
the Society's corrections or notes are stored here.

Retrieved 2026-09-27, stored unmodified as `finpr.json`: 83 books, including
17 apocryphal books that the ingest skips because they stay outside the
spine. That leaves 66 books, 1,189 chapters and 31,102 verses.

## Versification

This file follows the KJV versification: verse counts match the KJV in all
but 2 of 1,189 chapters (3 John 1, Revelation 12). Where the Finnish printed
numbering differs, mainly psalm superscriptions, the Finnish number is kept
inline in the text as `{51:2}`, so it is never lost.

## Verification

`John 14:6` in this edition reads:

> Jeesus sanoi hänelle: "Minä olen tie ja totuus ja elämä; ei kukaan tule
> Isän tykö muutoin kuin minun kauttani.

which matches the printed 1938 text.
