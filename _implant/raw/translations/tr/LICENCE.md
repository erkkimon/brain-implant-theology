# Textus Receptus (1550/1894) — licence

**Status: public domain. Free to redistribute.**

## The text

The Greek New Testament of the Reformation tradition: Stephanus's 1550
edition, in the form F. H. A. Scrivener printed in 1894 (the Greek text
underlying the King James Version). Both editions are far beyond any
copyright term, and raamattu.uskonkirjat.net lists it as *vapaassa
käytössä*, in free use (`../licence-audit.md`).

## This digital edition

Obtained from `scrollmapper/bible_databases`
(https://github.com/scrollmapper/bible_databases), `formats/json/TR.json`,
labelled "TR: Textus Receptus (1550/1894)". That repository distributes its
compilation under the **MIT Licence**. The text is unaccented lower-case
Greek, without breathings.

Retrieved 2026-09-27 and stored unmodified as `tr.json`. The file pads the
Old Testament with empty verses. The ingest skips empty verses, so only the
New Testament is used: 27 books, 260 chapters, verse counts identical to the
KJV in every chapter.

## Verification

`John 14:6` in this edition reads:

> λεγει αυτω ο ιησους εγω ειμι η οδος και η αληθεια και η ζωη ουδεις
> ερχεται προς τον πατερα ει μη δι εμου

which matches the Scrivener text.
